飛白 × 滲みthe blend — dry speed, wet rest

One brush, one physics. Where the hand moves fast the ink has no time to soak — the bristles tear white and the edge stays crisp. Where the brush presses and rests — the first touch, the final stop — water floods the paper and walks outward through the fibers on its own. Watch a stroke finish: the ink keeps moving after the hand has left.

紺紙金泥the same physics, in gold

The temple-sutra pairing — gold pigment on indigo paper — driven by the same living simulation. Gold granulates where it settles into the fiber; the wet front carries a faint shimmer past the edge before it dries.
