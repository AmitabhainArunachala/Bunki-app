Quests

preview on in this build only

From the word 職業 in something you are reading, make a quest: answer the readings of 職業, 知識 and 組織, check each one, and say whether you knew it. A quest in progress shows in Lessons. You can hold up to 8.

  • Open the essay with 職業 · preview

Then: tap 職業 in the first paragraph until its box opens (three taps), tap the word at the top of the box, and press “Make a quest from this word · preview”. On a first visit the app opens one word’s box by itself to show how; close that one first.

Rough. On the first day, and again after each rebuild of this prototype, the practice says “This answer couldn’t be recorded for review.” The quest still counts each word. A press on “Start quest” in the first instant after the card opens can be missed; press again.

The essay is 都市の匿名性と言語 (Urban Anonymity and Language), near the end of the bookshelf under N1 Literature. From search (Words, then 職業) the word’s page offers the practice and its two rooms, with no quest: a quest needs the sentence it came from.

Needs your hands

Built and reachable. Each waits for something only you can give.

  • Tutor, quiz, AI reading and Speak Each works once your own key is entered on this device (Settings, then Tutor connection; the Speak room has its own setup). They spend money.
  • One-tap problem reports “Report a problem” would send the report straight to the makers. As built, it opens a GitHub page where you finish sending by hand. It needs a running relay and its key. Reports and screenshots sent that way become public GitHub issues.
  • News headlines, file intake, device sync and native save These work only inside the Mac desktop app.
  • Offline use and home-screen install These need an https address.

Built but not connected yet

The code exists. Nothing in this build opens it.

  • Build a character On 職, 識 or 織, build the character from its two parts and keep up to 16 builds. being wired on 2026-11-10; its door moves up when it lands
  • Passage A fixed walk through seven stops, 職 → 戠 → 識 → 戠 → 織 → 糸 → 織, that can be interrupted and resumed. It has no screen yet. being wired on 2026-11-10; its door moves up when it lands
  • Orbit word field Words circling on five rings; each pass pulls a word toward the centre. being wired on 2026-11-10; its door moves up when it lands
  • The 110-piece library 110 more things to read: 57 Aozora, 30 Bunki originals, 15 Global Voices, 8 Wikipedia. Held for your selection; 9 texts have known typos and 5 have paragraphs marked for rewriting. being wired on 2026-11-10; its door moves up when it lands
  • A scene for any other kanji Only 門 has a scene and a picture. Each new one needs its own lesson and its own picture.
  • Eight contract modules One parts graph for every kanji; coming back to the exact word, scroll and draft; one dictionary entry shared by search and the word card; Speak corrections you can inspect and take back; one view of evidence by skill; one model for worlds; same-spelling words kept apart; every answer as one typed event. Each has tests and no connection to the app, and each would replace something that works today.
  • Library cuts A prepared decision to show 57 of the 156 shelf articles. It needs your recorded decision.
  • The old voice Retired by ruling on 2026-11-10. Three controls still promise sound and give none: the voice row in Settings, the 音 button on a review answer, and Listen on one article.
  • Card audio and two-device sync for the new study screen The audio release and the sync server are not in this build.
  • N2 full, medium and short tests with listening The questions and recordings are not in this build.
