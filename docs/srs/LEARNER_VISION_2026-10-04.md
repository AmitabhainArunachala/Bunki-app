## What this settles (read with `LEARNER_BRIEF_2026-10-04.md` and the research)

- **Card length:** a passage, not a sentence. Three to four medium-to-long sentences,
  one target per card, graded on the target. Massive-context cloze is optional, not
  the default. This is the "meet in the middle" between the AJATT research
  (one failure point per card) and the brief.
- **Repetition across textures:** the same word or kanji must recur in different
  passages, registers and topics, as separate cards spread over days.
- **AI-written passages are welcome** when original sources are unavailable, provided
  there are verification loops (naturalness, facts, level, one-target rule).
- **Level coverage:** passages should mix JLPT N3, N2 and N1 grammar and vocabulary
  deliberately, as reading-comprehension preparation.
- **Goals:** JLPT N1 at the next possible sitting; Kanken 1級 eventually; a Japanese
  YouTube channel speaking fluidly on history, Eastern philosophy, Advaita Vedanta,
  Indian and Japanese culture and other topics; "a master's degree" level of
  conversation in a few chosen fields.
- **Preferred input:** reading while listening to narrated books (脳に任せる,
  アルゴリズム, 半導体戦争, GRIT, 複利習慣 / Atomic Habits); YouTube (中田敦彦,
  パラノイア, Qさん and others). Words are noted while reading.
- **Asked for:** a way for the app's sensei to see the learner's YouTube history.
