## Register notes for mining

- **Best for everyday or colloquial vocab:** livedoor (独女通信, Peachy, livedoor HOMME, MOVIE ENTER, Sports Watch are chatty web-magazine prose), WRIME (casual SNS, but NC-ND), KWDLC (blogs and shop pages), GSD (reviews and blogs).
- **Best for formal or academic vocab:** KFTT (very large, but skewed to history, Buddhism and Kyoto; many proper nouns and archaic terms), JSQuAD/JaQuAD (broad Wikipedia topics), Wikinews.
- Duplicates: JSQuAD and JaQuAD may share some Wikipedia paragraphs, so dedupe across sources by exact string.
- livedoor quirks: lines 1–3 of each article are the URL, date and title, and the body starts at line 4. Bodies contain `【関連記事】` link lists, `■` headers and URLs, so drop those lines. smax/it-life-hack contain spec-sheet lines.
- KFTT quirks: it keeps parenthesised readings and alternates, e.g. `（五劫思惟（ごこうしゆい））`. Strip parentheses or prefer lines without them. Its sentences are long.
