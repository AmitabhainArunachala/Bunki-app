一 · 飛白the dry brush splits

Forty bristles, one dying ink load. Where the hand moves fast the brush runs dry and the stroke tears into white wind — 空海's flying-white, grown from real bristle depletion, never a texture stamp.

二 · 滲みwet ink keeps walking

Written on damp gasenshi: after the brush passes, the ink keeps travelling — feathering outward along the paper's fibers in tendrils, edges fraying like living things. The stroke breathes for two seconds after it lands.

三 · 拓本stone rubbing

The character as a thousand-year stele: paper pressed onto carved stone and rubbed with an ink ball. The glyph is the ABSENCE — white stone cut through charcoal black, edges chipped, surface pocked by the rubbing.

四 · 銀河墨the ink is a galaxy

The wild one, and the one that already knows this app: inside each stroke body drifts the 墨流し universe — star dust, slow nebulae. The kanji as a window cut into night. The corridor's own galaxy, held in a brush shape.

五 · 墨流しwritten on marbled water

Suminagashi — the oldest marbling on earth, and the app's own name. The sheet was pulled from a tray of floating ink rings; the character is written over the drifted rings in dense fresh sumi with a heavy bleed.
