# Audit and limitations

Date: **2026-09-28**. This audits a research/specification deliverable, not a built application.

## Completed verification

- Ten research tracks each produced a notes file with **Takeaway / Cited findings / Inferences / Gaps**.
- The synthesis covers sections **A–J**, including a **27-comparator × 12-capability** matrix. These counts are calculated from the delivered document structure, not market statistics.
- An engineering review identified nine implementation ambiguities; the specification now defines atomic idempotent confirmation, host-issued content verification, correction replay, assistance phases, placement/FSRS isolation, withdrawal propagation, milestone-1 release gating, executable policy predicates and inert content capture/import.
- The follow-up identified two more issues. Both are addressed in the final specification: confirmation predicates include new query membership/absence; dictionary identity migrations require explicit equivalence and do not spread evidence across ambiguous splits/merges. These are **addressed in specification, not implemented or integration-tested**.
- The speech/platform reviewer reconciled the chosen costs, licenses and egress boundaries. A missing MFA dictionary citation was added.
- Cost arithmetic was independently recomputed using decimal arithmetic; the stated scenario totals agree. Assumptions, exclusions and source rates are in **COST_MODEL.json**.
- Structural and rendering checks are described in the final validation receipt. They do not establish product functionality or linguistic accuracy.

## Material limits

1. **No repository audit:** existing Bunki capabilities/counts are user-supplied. Current branches, private local work, test gates, exact FSRS package and existing asset rights remain UNVERIFIED. Milestone 1 performs that audit before changing code/data.
2. **Documentation-based competitor teardowns:** no paid account purchase, scripted live product test or checkout was performed. Memory durability, exact proprietary models, export completeness and feature parity can require a later hands-on evaluation. A U cell means UNVERIFIED, not absent.
3. **Mutable sources:** the campaign prefers recent primary sources but includes foundational older research and undated live documentation. The access date is 2026-09-28; a live README is not an immutable historical snapshot. No archive has been manufactured. Version/commit capture is a production gate.
4. **Price uncertainty:** currency/billing ambiguity, multiple store SKUs, promotions and unavailable official prices are labeled. The retrieved official Sonnet pages agree on the used rate; a stale indexed excerpt is retained as a discrepancy note, not treated as a competing current quote.
5. **No efficacy established:** meta-analyses, individual trials, vendor-funded reports and product claims are distinguished. Effect sizes from mathematics, physics, Spanish or English cannot establish adult Japanese-learning benefit. Bunki requires its own delayed, unassisted transfer study.
6. **No local-model benchmark:** Japanese diagnostic accuracy, pronunciation assessment, generated-language quality, target-device latency, memory and battery behavior remain UNVERIFIED. Conditional choices are not achieved release gates.
7. **Licensing/compliance is asset- and implementation-specific:** the sources support release decisions, not blanket legal clearance. Research-only/NC/unclear assets are excluded unless permission is obtained; a code license does not confer media rights. Store approval and final legal classification remain open gates.
8. **No law amendment:** continuous cloud tutoring, speech, background mining and sync remain disabled under the export-only law. The conditional economics are not authorization to enable those paths.
9. **No universal uniqueness claim:** this research cannot prove that no competing product implements a capability. It gives testable Bunki commitments and an evidence-labeled comparison.

## Evidence conventions

External factual claims use URLs and dates in the relevant notes/specification. Missing publication dates are labeled undated. **PROPOSED/DESIGN** marks original engineering choices; **ESTIMATE/CALCULATED** marks planning assumptions or arithmetic. The owner's supplied requirements have a date but no invented public URL. Source indices include attempted/blocked links only when the associated passage explicitly labels them.

The engineering review appendix preserves its earlier snapshots and findings as a review history. This final disposition supersedes an earlier “remaining” label for the two refinements explicitly resolved above. No acceptance test in the specification should be read as already passing in Bunki.
