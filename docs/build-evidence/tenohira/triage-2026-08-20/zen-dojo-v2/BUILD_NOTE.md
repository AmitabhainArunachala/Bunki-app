# 禅道場 v2 — the hypnotic training zone (operator, 2026-08-20)

The operator's word, verbatim in intent: _a tad better, still 3/10. As
futuristic as possible while keeping the zen dojo feel. Audio must be
accessible on each and every backside answer card. Minimal with more
depth and texture. The backside is still crowded and confusing. Research
this madly — 10 agents, cutting edge. A hypnotic training zone that
feels like 1600 Japan on a 2335 advanced device._

## The research fleet

Ten agents ran (nine lanes + one synthesis director). The workflow's
structured-output channel was broken session-wide (the sandbox's
permission handler stripped every subagent tool input), so no lane could
fetch live pages — each delivered dense findings from retained knowledge
as text, harvested from the transcripts into this directory (one .md per
lane). All hex/contrast values were hand-computed against WCAG 2.x and
are re-checkable.

## What was built from it

- **The stage is urushi lacquer at night**: warm near-black radial
  (#17120E→#0E0C0A→#060505, never #000), an andon light pool above the
  card, 青海波 waves rising from the bottom of the glass (gold at 9%
  alpha, stage only). The generated paper canvas rests in zen.
- **The card is real washi**: kōzo-fiber turbulence + su-no-me laid
  lines (texture alpha ≤6%), maki-e gold rim, a layered shadow ramp
  under one 12-o'clock light, lacquerware radii (8px).
- **The type is 1600's**: Shippori Mincho B1 800 for the word
  (char-count-adaptive size/tracking, scroll-like aki), Yuji Syuku —
  a real shodō hand — for the kana reading in bengara iron-oxide red
  (7.0:1 on washi), Kaisei Tokumin 800 for the 再難良易 seals via a
  4-glyph micro-subset. Google Fonts on the web deploy; offline falls
  back to Hiragino/Yu Mincho by design.
- **The back decrowded by the minimum-information rule**: ONE primary
  sense decides the grade (18px, upright); the next two whisper on one
  muted line; everything else — the example sentences and late senses —
  waits behind ONE named fold (`▸ 詳しく · 例文 2 …`) under a kintsugi
  gold seam. The sentences are never absent: the fold counts them, the
  probe opens them, the tokens stay ladder-live.
- **音 on every answer card**: a fifth seal (音 in a hairline ring)
  beside the reading speaks the KANA via the device voice — kana is
  deterministic where rare kanji misread. Speaking state is color +
  a static ring, no motion noise. The operator's 2026-08-20 word
  explicitly supersedes the earlier word-audio hold; the door names
  itself 仮 in its label until PR 五's judged voice.
- **The ceremony**: the answer condenses out of the paper (blur→ink,
  520ms, cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)) in reading order — reading at
  +120ms, sense +210ms, seam +300ms, fold +390ms, seals last at
  480–660ms. The room breathes at 6/min (the HRV-resonance rate) — in
  LIGHT, not geometry: a transform breath made every element inside the
  card perpetually unstable to the battery browser's actionability
  checks, so the paper holds still and its aura breathes (opacity-only
  pseudo-element). Full reduced-motion fallback: 150ms fades, no
  stagger, no blur, aura off.
- **Two contrast laws enforced**: gold exists only on lacquer; reds and
  sumi only on washi. Small gold text uses solid #A58A4E (5.0:1), never
  alpha-dimmed gold (3.5:1 — caught before the battery did).

## Honesty ledger

- The examples-behind-fold changes the older "a card never opens onto
  zero examples" ruling: the operator's 2026-08-20 direction supersedes
  it. The probe was rewritten to open the named fold and demand the same
  living tokens — equal strength, new shape — plus a new probe pinning
  the 音 door to every answer face.
- Fonts are SELF-HOSTED (prototypes/corridor/fonts/, 181 unicode-range
  woff2 chunks ≈ 7MB repo weight, OFL 1.1 — see fonts/OFL-NOTE.md). The
  first battery run convicted the Google-Fonts links: every "no console
  errors" probe failed on ERR_CONNECTION_RESET in the no-network
  verifier browser (216/224 · 45/46). Self-hosting was the correct fix,
  not an accommodation — the corridor now makes zero runtime network
  requests, offline and standalone fall back to system mincho, and
  unicode-range keeps per-card runtime cost to the chunks actually
  shown. Yuji Syuku ships kana chunks only (the reading is always kana).
- The world-picker palettes still tint every other room; the zen room
  now keeps its own fixed night materials by design.
