## Publication and device status

All 30 have had two AI editorial passes covering Japanese phrasing, source
boundaries, safety, requested form, and cross-article similarity. That is not
the required human review. Every sidecar record remains
`humanReviewStatus:"pending"`, and `verify-native-readings.py --publication`
therefore fails intentionally. Public publication is blocked until a human
Japanese editor reviews grammar, theology, historical claims, tone, and all
generated ruby.

The automated similarity tripwire compares the 30 authored texts with one
another (longest shared normalized run: 29 characters). It does not certify a
complete V1–V4 transcript-to-article comparison; source-level similarity and
presenter-imitation review remains part of the human publication gate. V5 was
unavailable and must not be represented as audited.

The Chromium verifier is a 390×844 touch-emulation gate, not a real-iPhone
receipt. A cache-busted physical-iPhone N3/N2/N1 walk remains an external gate
and must not be inferred from desktop Chromium emulation.
