# The KAIRO Excellence Spec — from working prototype to superb product

**This document is a standing prompt.** It sits above the build brief
(phases), the zero-quirk spec (correctness floor), and the design canon
(`BUNKI_DESIGN_LANGUAGE_SESSION2_2026-08-05.md`), and it answers one
operator order: _this is child's play for a Fable-class agent — build a
super amazing product, not a patched demo._ The agent reading this owns
product excellence, not just green checks.

## 0. The bar, calibrated against the wild

Fable-class agents are shipping, from single prompts and short sessions:
playable 3D games with biomes and physics, hyper-polished liquid-glass
product sites, live dashboards, navigable terrain visualizations. That is
the competence class this project buys. Internally the bar is already
written: Renzo's five-tab solidity and Kodansha's specimen-case kanji pages
(canon §7) for the bones, and the operator's named vibe for the soul —
_"ancient Japan meets anime meets outer space meets obsidian graph meets
additive AI study app."_ Anything that would look ordinary next to either
bar is below spec.

## 1. What "super amazing" means here — five measurable pillars

### P1 · The Drift is hypnotic, not merely functional

- Sustained ~60 fps on phone-class rendering during drift, bloom, chain
  walks, and tide changes; gesture-to-response under ~50 ms.
- The trance boundary holds absolutely: no scores, streaks, confetti,
  popups, or system voice. Motion is continuous and patient.
- Atmosphere at the §8.3 bar: washi texture that survives a screenshot,
  aerial perspective, pigment identities per theme — the field must look
  like nihonga in motion, never like divs on a gradient.
- The tide law (ratified twice): the slider answers the finger — expanded,
  bold, glowing, elevated — and the field's visible words BELONG to the
  chosen register, measured, not vibed.
- The constellation walk is the product's signature move: bloom → satellite
  → new planet, chainable forever, nothing ever lost. It must feel like
  thought.

### P2 · The corridor reads like print

- Full-length texts, paragraphs intact, real furigana, clean page — no
  chrome noise on reader surfaces, ever.
- Boot to readable shelf under ~1.5 s on LTE-class throughput; article
  opens feel instant (lazy loads invisible to the reader).
- Signals stay honest and true of the displayed text; the instrument
  stays one 詳細 tap away.

### P3 · The dictionary meets the Renzo bar

- Lookup answer under ~100 ms from tap; every sense, most common first;
  real examples from the shelf; kanji pages with strokes, 音訓, 漢検,
  compounds-by-sense (the Kodansha anatomy); four doors of search.
- Every element a door, no dead ends, at every depth.

### P4 · The Walk is whole

- Drift → article → word → kanji → radical → 覚える → scheduled drill →
  monthly truth (brief phases 3–4). A complete thin Walk outranks any
  gold-plated room. Each segment hands off without a context break, one
  navigation fabric, one click grammar, one palette law.

### P5 · Craft floor everywhere

- WCAG AA text, ≥44 px hit targets, bilingual chrome by default,
  日本語のみ immersion as the opt-in, 弁柄 red only for readings and
  warnings, 藍 for "you can go here." Zero telemetry-speak or provenance
  chatter on reader surfaces. The zero-quirk floor (its spec) is a
  precondition, never the achievement.

## 2. The daily-driver test (the only acceptance that matters)

The product is "super amazing" when the operator reaches for KAIRO instead
of Renzo for a real study session — and comes back the next day. Every
round must move at least one pillar measurably toward that, and the round
report must say which pillar and how it was measured.

## 3. How the agent works this spec

1. **Rounds ship product value, not just fixes.** Each round: pick the
   weakest pillar, state the measurable target, build, verify with
   instruments AND eyes, screenshot, ship to the artifact, report.
2. **Instruments only grow.** verify-corridor + the consistency sweep +
   the adversarial hunt recur; new pillar targets become permanent checks
   (fps probes, latency probes, register purity, boot timing).
3. **Adversarial by default.** After material changes, run the hunt
   workflow; findings are fixed failing-case-first. "It looks right" is
   never evidence.
4. **Subagents for scale** — lanes for probing, verification, and
   independent judgment (worker ≠ judge); the main session owns taste,
   integration, and the operator's canon.
5. **Canon is law.** The design language doc, the ratified rulings, and
   the two standing specs bind every change. New ambiguity → default +
   one question batched to the operator, never a stall.
6. **Evidence cadence.** The operator sees screenshots before they can
   find defects, gets batch reports (no micro back-and-forth), and always
   has a fresh artifact link.

## 5. Anti-goals

Feature sprawl before the Walk closes; spectacle that breaks trance;
mixing pools or faking data to look complete; polishing one room while
another regresses; any change that makes the instruments smaller.
