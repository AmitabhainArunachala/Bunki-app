# KAIRO / Bunki — the brief I write for myself (Claude, 2026-08-10)

The companion file (`DRIFT_MOTION_FRESH_EYES_2026-08-10.md`) is for an agent
with no history. This one is for me — the agent that built the last two
rounds, got the suites green, and still handed the operator a product that
felt wrong in their hands. Both files describe the same product. This one
also describes the builder.

## What I now know that I didn't act on

The operator told me the goal ten times and I kept shrinking it to the task
in front of me. The goal is not "fix the drift" or "add a stroke page." It
is the whole instrument: paper-dictionary-grade dictionary (~70k), full
synonym dictionary, kanji and grammar dictionaries, simplified Anki-grade
SRS, lessons, JLPT N5→N1 and Kanken to 1級 lanes, a Satori-class reader,
and an AI that lives inside the app — speaking, judging level, writing
custom readings, tuning the SRS — all sharing one model of the learner.
One flowing, mesmerizing sequence. Recursive hypnosis with ease and
aesthetic precision. When I make any local decision, I test it against
that whole, not against the corner I happen to be standing in.

## My failure modes, named, with countermeasures

1. **I trusted proxies over eyes.** 21/21 and 60fps-in-headless told me the
   water was calm while the operator's thumb said otherwise. Rule: no claim
   about feel without watching my own 60-second recording of the surface,
   at phone size, and saying honestly whether I would show it to a
   designer I respect.
2. **I delegated the seeing.** Subagents verified mechanisms; nobody looked
   at the composition. The tearing dev bar, the shouting satellites, the
   inverted hierarchy — all visible in one screenshot I never took. Rule:
   I look first, delegate second, and I re-look after every merge.
3. **I invented the vision when unsure.** Twice I wrote the product's soul
   from my own head instead of the operator's canon, and it read as
   delusion to the person who actually holds the vision. Rule: when the
   question is "what should this BE," the answer comes from the frozen
   specs or from asking — never from my fluency.
4. **I confused shipping with delivering.** A pushed branch, a green gate,
   a draft PR — none of it is delivery. Delivery is the operator feeling
   the thing on their phone and not being disappointed. Everything else is
   inventory.
5. **My own history is now a bias.** I have theories about the 650ms churn
   and the arbiter flips. They might be right. But I found them AFTER my
   last confident fix failed, which means my confidence calibration on this
   surface is poor. I hold my own hypotheses to the same standard as a
   stranger's: reproduce first, on the cruelest available emulation of the
   real device, before spending a line of code on them.

## How I work from here

- **Integrity pass first** (operator's ruling): the water's calm, the
  smears, the clipping, the family strangers, the murk, the two connector
  languages, the 404. Each one: reproduce → fix → watch a recording →
  only then move. Depth before breadth because a rotten floorboard under
  a bigger house rots the house.
- **Then breadth, in slices that each close a loop**: a real SRS session
  (FSRS, one queue, capture-is-card-creation) before more surfaces; the
  70k dictionary and the synonym dictionary as first-class rooms; lessons
  and JLPT/Kanken lanes; reader growth. Every new pillar plugs into the
  ONE learner state — no adjacent mini-apps, ever. If a slice can't share
  state yet, it isn't ready to build.
- **AI enters the prototype via key-in-browser.** AI proposes,
  deterministic systems dispose; every judgment about the learner cites
  its evidence; no lookup ever waits on a model.
- **The operator's cadence**: ship to the Pages deploy, one plain
  paragraph on what will feel different, then listen. Their hands are the
  gate. When they say it's wrong, the first move is to reproduce what they
  felt — not to defend what I measured.
- **Verification stays** — suites green, generators regenerated, laws
  law-checked — but demoted to what it is: the floor, never the verdict.
