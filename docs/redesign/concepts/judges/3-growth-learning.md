## The finding that cuts across all four: Today spoils a due card

In all four, Today's "word of the day" is one of today's due cards, shown with reading or meaning minutes before recall. That turns retrieval into recognition, inflates the FSRS grade, and stretches the interval on a memory never retrieved.

- **D**, by spec ("the day word comes from the scheduler's next due card"): 推進 "promotion, driving forward", then stop 3 "Walk the web 推進→進→隹", then card 01/38 is 推進.
- **B**: "WORD OF THE DAY · DUE TODAY · SEEN 6×" over 記憶 "memory, recollection" and its glossed parts.
- **A**: 研ぐ "to sharpen, to hone", and the "Arriving today" train lists all 12 due words.
- **C**: 推進 すいしん with "Follow the bird" into its full web; 推進 is in the session's list.

**Rule:** the day's word is never due today. Use yesterday's passed word, or an unreviewed word today's article holds (a hook into Read). Or show only glyph and hook ("a small bird hides inside both kanji") and unlock meaning and dive after the cards, as the reward. Show the due count, not the due words.

**Grades.** All four ship Again/Hard/Good/Easy; the engine draft names "Again and Good". For one-target passage cards a two-button default is more honest (Hard is the most misused FSRS button), faster, and halves decision cost. Hard/Easy behind a setting; pad intervals must come from FSRS, not fixtures.

**True minutes.** D's "38 cards · 9 min" is 14 s per 196-character N1 passage. A's "12 due · 4 new" beside "Begin 12 cards" is ambiguous. Over-promising breaks trust by day 2.

**Missing everywhere:** day 0 (every Today assumes day 41 or 214; C's sky is empty for a new user), a premium preview and paywall, and a share frame, all of which VISION §7 asks for now.

## A 磨き Migaki: 47

**Reasoning.** This is the most buildable and the most "his app, polished". The Today line (Cards → Read · 伊勢の時間 → Focus · 隹 family → Tomorrow · 9 due · first 怠…) is the best ritual diagram of the four: three stops, then a station that is already tomorrow, which builds the D1 pull into the morning screen. The card front is clean ("RECALL THE MARKED WORD: READING AND MEANING", target in 朱, no ruby in the DOM). "Twelve cards, whole." with "10 held · 2 again" is honest, and "The next door: 都市の匿名性と言語 · holds 5 of today's words" closes the loop. Me is the most honest progress screen: "N1 vocabulary 1,284 / 3,410 · held by recall 37.7%". The "Mended this month" kintsugi rewards recovery rather than attendance. "A line you copied by hand" in Yuji Syuku is the best share object in any concept. Against it: Today is the busiest first screen (the self-critique says so), the 3-second read is date → vertical word → passage → train → line → button, and the done screen leaves two-thirds of the phone empty below the next door, which wastes the moment of highest motivation.

**Graft:** (1) the Today line with a Tomorrow station; (2) "A line you copied by hand" as the 一句 share card; (3) Me's "held by recall" labelling on every horizon.
**Worst:** (1) 研ぐ meaning plus the due train shown before review; (2) Today density, which fails Musashi; (3) the done screen is half empty and offers no second door or tomorrow promise beyond one line.

## B 夜の文机 Night Desk: 46

**Reasoning.** B is the only concept whose voice is real: per-sentence Ami audio, a scan-line under the spoken word, and "Resume at sentence 3 · 3/13" on the shelf. For read-while-listen as the core method, that is the strongest Read room of the four. The shelf ranks by "your due words" with a count ("7 due words"), and the done screen hands off cleanly: "Today's review is done · 18 cards · 16 recalled · 2 again" → "Next door · 7 of today's words live in this article · Read it now" → "Tomorrow's first word 象…". That is the exact cards → reading loop. Me is honest and humane: "Your voice · 23 sentences shadowed · no target; a record", and "Words that came back · lost, then kept". Against it: the Today plate leaks 記憶 outright. The word sheet for a saved word shows "Open its web / Back to the sentence", so check that an unsaved word gets a one-tap Keep. Day mode is beige, and by its own critique it has no city, so it is the least alluring on a first open. Its walk (記憶 → 記 → 己) is correct but not jaw-dropping.

**Graft:** (1) real sentence audio, scan-line and "Resume at sentence N"; (2) "Your voice: a record, no target" as the output metric; (3) the session-end layout (tally, next door, Read it now, tomorrow's word half-shown).
**Worst:** (1) "DUE TODAY" word of the day with its meaning shown; (2) day Today is flat beige, the weakest first impression; (3) the web is one ring deep, so it shows less recursion than A or C.

## C 生きた本棚 Living Shelf: 49

**Reasoning.** C passes the 3-second test best: one huge word, then one panel reading "12 cards · 1 article · 1 question" with a progress rail, "Begin today · 12 cards · 6 min", and "Then 伊勢の時間 · 9 of your words". The triad (retrieve, read, speak to the sensei) is the right daily unit, and it is the only Today that names the sensei step. The red dots on due words in the sky put "your memory" where it can be seen. C is also the only concept where *misses travel*. The done screen ("The surface is clear.", Kept 10 · Again 2 · 6:12) says "Coming back in ten minutes, in new sentences: しめた・目下". That is VISION §6.2 point 5 and the best learning design in the set. Then: "Next door · 伊勢の時間 · Holds 9 of your words · Kore reads it in 4 min · Read it now", and "Tomorrow · 9 cards · first 推…". "Follow the bird", where 推進 → 推 → 隹 → "21 of its 40 kanji are already in your words", is the most shareable clip in the four concepts.  Against it: the Today word leaks a due card through the bird dive. The sky with about 80 words is noise for a beginner and empty on day 1. Me mixes honest and soft metrics: "412 words in review" and "1,220 kanji seen · Kanken 1" count exposure, not recall. Focus, tests and sensei exist only as rows. Ruby spreads the base text on the card back (包 丁, 職 人).

**Graft (from C into anything):** (1) "Coming back in ten minutes, in new sentences"; (2) the 12 / 1 / 1 Today triad; (3) "Follow the bird" with "N of its family already in your words" as the share clip.
**Worst:** (1) the bird dive spoils a due card; (2) sky density and the empty sky on day 1; (3) "seen" and "in review" metrics beside recall metrics, which breaks law 7.

## D 白紙 Hakushi: 39

**Reasoning.** D is clear and crisp: "Today's line · 3 stops · 17 min" with a minutes column is the most legible plan, and the Line navigation is a good idea. It has two pieces of learning design the evolutions lack. One is "Reading · characters read unaided 31,402 of 100,000", the best single north-star learning metric in any concept and close to VISION §7.6's "words read that were unknown thirty days earlier". The other is the year grid's "gold days = 6 days a lost word came back", which rewards recovery instead of streaks. The slip's "4× in this article · 21 passages in your decks" plus Keep with a seal is the clearest capture. But D's loop is built around the leak (see above). It has no session-end screen, so the pull after cards is undesigned. "38 due · 12 new" in 9 minutes is a dishonest estimate and a D7 killer. The GitHub-style year heatmap is the most streak-like object in the set. It is also the hardest to build into the corridor.

**Graft:** (1) "characters read unaided" as the north-star metric on Me; (2) gold days for a lost word coming back; (3) the slip footer "N× in this article · N passages in your decks" plus Keep.
**Worst:** (1) the day's word is the next due card, by spec; (2) no session end; (3) a 50-card day with a 9-minute promise.

## D1 / D7 / D30, day 0, premium

- **D1** is won at the end of session one: C and B hand off best, A undersells, D has nothing. **D7** needs short honest sessions: two buttons, true minutes, a backlog-aware new-card cap (D's 38 + 12 buries a week-1 user). **D30** is meaning: misses in new contexts (C), words that came back (B, C), the copied line (A), characters read unaided (D). No guilt anywhere, which is right; use draft-market's ink stone (硯) in place of every hanko grid.
- **Day 0 (90 s):** pick a level by reading three passages (N3/N2/N1), pick fields, read one voiced paragraph where every tap becomes a star, so the sky is born from your taps. The first session is 5 cards from those taps, so the first retrieval succeeds; the done screen promises tomorrow. Anki import is the second door (the N1 wedge).
- **Premium:** a shelf spine with a thin 金継ぎ seam; opening it plays the first voiced paragraph over its woodblock, then one washi page with $12/$96, Restore, and "Not now" the same size as Subscribe. Due reviews are never behind it.
