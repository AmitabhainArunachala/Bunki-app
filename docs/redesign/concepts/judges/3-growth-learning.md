## The finding that cuts across all four: Today spoils a due card

Every concept puts a "word of the day" on Today, and in every concept that word is one of today's due cards. In each case its reading or meaning is shown minutes before the learner is asked to recall it. This undoes the testing effect at the exact moment it should work: a recall right after exposure is recognition, the FSRS grade it produces is inflated, and the scheduler then stretches the interval on a memory that was never retrieved.

- **D** does it by design. SPEC: "The day word comes from the scheduler's next due card." Today shows 推進 with "promotion, driving forward", its reading and both kanji. "Walk the web: 推進 → 進 → 隹" is stop 3 of the line, and card **01/38** is 推進. The motion journey runs that leak in order.
- **B**: Today labels 記憶 "WORD OF THE DAY · DUE TODAY · SEEN 6×", then shows "memory, recollection", its kanji, and its parts with English glosses.
- **A**: 研ぐ "to sharpen, to hone" is the hero, and 研ぐ is also in the "Arriving today" train. That train lists all 12 due words before the session, which is a smaller version of the same priming.
- **C**: 推進 with すいしん is the hero, and "Follow the bird" opens its full web (meaning: "promotion"). 推進 is the first word in the session-end list, so it is due.

**Rule for the build:** the day's word is never a card due today. Use a word that was due *yesterday and passed* (a victory lap), or a word that is not yet in review and that today's article holds (a hook into Read). Alternatively, show only the glyph and the hook ("a small bird hides inside both kanji") and hold the meaning until the cards are done. The dive then becomes the reward at the end of the session, so Today reads "earn the door" rather than "here is the answer". Do not show the due-word train before the session either; show the count.

**Second cross-cutting fix: grades.** All four ship Again / Hard / Good / Easy. VISION §6.3 and the engine draft say "Again and Good" is the Anki floor. For passage cards graded on one target, a two-button default is more honest (Hard is the most misused button in FSRS data and lowers retention estimates), it is faster, and it halves the decision cost per card. Hard and Easy can go behind a setting. The interval labels on the pads (1m / 8m / 3d / 9d) are illustrative in A, B and C, and the build must take them from FSRS.

**Third: time estimates must be true.** D's "Begin · 38 cards · 9 min" is about 14 seconds per 196-character N1 passage, which is not possible. A's "12 due · 4 new" sits beside "Begin 12 cards", which leaves the count unclear. Under-promising the minutes is the cheapest retention lever there is, and over-promising breaks trust on day 2.

**Fourth: nobody designed day 1, premium or a share.** Every Today assumes day 214 or day 41. C's sky is "your words" and is empty for a new user. None of the four has a paywall page, a premium preview or a share frame, although VISION §7 says to "design them into the rooms now".

## A 磨き Migaki: 47

**Reasoning.** This is the most buildable and the most "his app, polished". The Today line (Cards → Read · 伊勢の時間 → Focus · 隹 family → Tomorrow · 9 due · first 怠…) is the best ritual diagram of the four: three stops, then a station that is already tomorrow, which builds the D1 pull into the morning screen. The card front is clean ("RECALL THE MARKED WORD: READING AND MEANING", target in 朱, no ruby in the DOM). "Twelve cards, whole." with "10 held · 2 again" is honest, and "The next door: 都市の匿名性と言語 · holds 5 of today's words" closes the loop. Me is the most honest progress screen: "N1 vocabulary 1,284 / 3,410 · held by recall 37.7%". The "Mended this month" kintsugi rewards recovery rather than attendance. "A line you copied by hand" in Yuji Syuku is the best share object in any concept. Against it: Today is the busiest first screen (the self-critique says so), the 3-second read is date → vertical word → passage → train → line → button, and the done screen leaves two-thirds of the phone empty below the next door, which wastes the moment of highest motivation.

**Graft:** (1) the Today line with a Tomorrow station; (2) "A line you copied by hand" as the 一句 share card; (3) Me's "held by recall" labelling on every horizon.
**Worst:** (1) 研ぐ meaning plus the due train shown before review; (2) Today density, which fails Musashi; (3) the done screen is half empty and offers no second door or tomorrow promise beyond one line.

## B 夜の文机 Night Desk: 46

**Reasoning.** B is the only concept whose voice is real: per-sentence Ami audio, a scan-line under the spoken word, and "Resume at sentence 3 · 3/13" on the shelf. For read-while-listen as the core method, that is the strongest Read room of the four. The shelf ranks by "your due words" with a count ("7 due words"), and the done screen hands off cleanly: "Today's review is done · 18 cards · 16 recalled · 2 again" → "Next door · 7 of today's words live in this article · Read it now" → "Tomorrow's first word 象…". That is the exact cards → reading loop. Me is honest and humane: "Your voice · 23 sentences shadowed · no target; a record", and "Words that came back · lost, then kept". The haptic tick and the vermilion slash on Again give a wrong answer a body. Against it: the Today plate leaks 記憶 outright. The word sheet for a saved word shows "Open its web / Back to the sentence", so check that an unsaved word gets a one-tap Keep. Day mode is beige, and by its own critique it has no city, so it is the least alluring on a first open. Its walk (記憶 → 記 → 己) is correct but not jaw-dropping.

**Graft:** (1) real sentence audio, scan-line and "Resume at sentence N"; (2) "Your voice: a record, no target" as the output metric; (3) the session-end layout (tally, next door, Read it now, tomorrow's word half-shown).
**Worst:** (1) "DUE TODAY" word of the day with its meaning shown; (2) day Today is flat beige, the weakest first impression; (3) the web is one ring deep, so it shows less recursion than A or C.

## C 生きた本棚 Living Shelf: 49

**Reasoning.** C passes the 3-second test best: one huge word, then one panel reading "12 cards · 1 article · 1 question" with a progress rail, "Begin today · 12 cards · 6 min", and "Then 伊勢の時間 · 9 of your words". The triad (retrieve, read, speak to the sensei) is the right daily unit, and it is the only Today that names the sensei step. The red dots on due words in the sky put "your memory" where it can be seen. C is also the only concept where *misses travel*. The done screen ("The surface is clear.", Kept 10 · Again 2 · 6:12) says "Coming back in ten minutes, in new sentences: しめた・目下". That is VISION §6.2 point 5, and the best single line of learning design in the set: a lapse comes back in a fresh context, not as the same card. Then: "Next door · 伊勢の時間 · Holds 9 of your words · Kore reads it in 4 min · Read it now", and "Tomorrow · 9 cards · first 推…". "Follow the bird", where 推進 → 推 → 隹 → "21 of its 40 kanji are already in your words", is the most shareable clip in the four concepts. It is awe at Japanese with the learner's own number in it, which suits both a Short and an ASO screenshot. Against it: the Today word leaks a due card through the bird dive. The sky with about 80 words is noise for a beginner and empty on day 1. Me mixes honest and soft metrics: "412 words in review" and "1,220 kanji seen · Kanken 1" count exposure, not recall. Focus, tests and sensei exist only as rows. Ruby spreads the base text on the card back (包 丁, 職 人).

**Graft (from C into anything):** (1) "Coming back in ten minutes, in new sentences"; (2) the 12 / 1 / 1 Today triad; (3) "Follow the bird" with "N of its family already in your words" as the share clip.
**Worst:** (1) the bird dive spoils a due card; (2) sky density and the empty sky on day 1; (3) "seen" and "in review" metrics beside recall metrics, which breaks law 7.

## D 白紙 Hakushi: 39

**Reasoning.** D is clear and crisp: "Today's line · 3 stops · 17 min" with a minutes column is the most legible plan, and the Line navigation is a good idea. It has two pieces of learning design the evolutions lack. One is "Reading · characters read unaided 31,402 of 100,000", the best single north-star learning metric in any concept and close to VISION §7.6's "words read that were unknown thirty days earlier". The other is the year grid's "gold days = 6 days a lost word came back", which rewards recovery instead of streaks. The slip's "4× in this article · 21 passages in your decks" plus Keep with a seal is the clearest capture. But D's loop is built around the leak (see above). It has no session-end screen, so the pull after cards is undesigned. "38 due · 12 new" in 9 minutes is a dishonest estimate and a D7 killer. The GitHub-style year heatmap is the most streak-like object in the set. As a from-scratch track it is the hardest to build into the corridor.

**Graft:** (1) "characters read unaided" as the north-star metric on Me; (2) gold days for a lost word coming back; (3) the slip footer "N× in this article · N passages in your decks" plus Keep.
**Worst:** (1) the day's word is the next due card, by spec; (2) no session end; (3) a 50-card day with a 9-minute promise.

## Retention read (my estimate, for argument rather than as a forecast)

- **D1** is won at the end of the first session, not on the first screen. C and B have the strongest handoffs (next door, Read it now, tomorrow's first word half-shown). A has it but undersells it, and D has none.
- **D7** is won by sessions that stay short and honest, and by the feeling that reading gets easier. That requires two-button grades, true minutes, a new-card cap that reacts to backlog (D's 38 + 12 would bury a week-1 user), and the shelf's "N of your words" count rising visibly.
- **D30** is won by meaning: misses returning in new contexts (C), "Words that came back" (B, C), the copied line (A), and characters read unaided (D). None of the four uses guilt, which is correct. Keep the ink stone (硯) from draft-market as the streak substitute; it beats every hanko grid, because a missed day leaves the hollow intact.

## Onboarding (missing everywhere): what the build must add

Day 0 should take about 90 seconds: pick a level by reading, not by a quiz (three passages at N3, N2 and N1, then "which could you mostly follow?"); pick your fields; then one voiced paragraph in which every word you tap becomes a star. The sky is born from your own taps. First card session: 5 cards from words you just tapped, so the first retrieval succeeds. Then the done screen: "Tomorrow · 5 cards · first 〜". Import from Anki is the wedge for the N1 buyer (draft-market §2) and belongs on this path as a second door.

## Premium, tastefully (missing everywhere)

Put it where C already has a natural slot. On the shelf, a premium book spine carries a thin 金継ぎ seam. Opening it plays the first voiced paragraph in full over its woodblock, then shows one washi page: what you get, $12 / $96, Restore, and "Not now" the same size as Subscribe. The session-end "next door" can sometimes be a premium article, but only when a free one with as many of the learner's words does not exist. Due reviews are never behind it.

## Pick and grafts

**Build C.** It has the best Today (the triad), the best session end (misses travel, next door, tomorrow), and the best share moment (the bird). It also has the deepest recursion. Graft into it:

1. **The fix:** the day's word is never due today; the dive is the after-session reward; show no due-word list before the session.
2. **From B:** real sentence audio with the scan-line, "Resume at sentence N", "Hear" on Today's sentence, the haptic tick and the Again slash, and "Your voice: a record, no target".
3. **From A:** the Today line's Tomorrow station, "held by recall" on every horizon (replacing C's "seen" and "in review"), "A line you copied by hand" as the daily 一句 share card, and the togidashi reveal if it tests faster than C's held beat.
4. **From D:** "characters read unaided" as Me's headline metric, gold days for recovered words instead of a plain stamp grid, and the slip footer with Keep and its seal.
5. **New to all:** two-button default grading, true minutes, a backlog-aware new-card cap, a day-0 path that births the sky from the learner's own taps, one premium preview and paywall page, and share frames for the bird dive, the copied line and 今年の漢字.
