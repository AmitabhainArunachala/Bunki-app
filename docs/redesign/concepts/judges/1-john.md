## A 磨き Migaki: 46.5

A is the sword polisher's version, and it shows. On `today-day`, the vertical 研ぐ in Shippori sits beside its 縦書き passage, which reads like a real Japanese page. The Me book (`me-day`, `me-book`) has a gold kintsugi seam on 怠る/目下/辛抱 and a hand-copied line in Yuji Syuku. It is the most moving progress screen of the four, and it speaks straight to Hakuin's compassion and his "a page I couldn't read last month". The 研ぐ→研→石→磨 walk in `motion-frames` is the one he would screenshot for the channel, because migaki is the name of the concept. A's localisation is the most careful: KANKEN 8 in English, 漢検8級 in 日本語. It is the easiest port.

His eye would still catch three things.
1. **The sky is demoted.** His door is now a faint band of grey words at the top of Today (`today-day`). It is "kept" on paper, but the hush is gone, and he defended exactly that hush.
2. **Today is crowded.** The self-critique admits it: date, meta, the vertical word, the passage, the train of six word chips, the four-station line and the button all fight. That is "everythign strewn togther" on the first screen.
3. **The day sheet is still mostly cream with a black tab band.** At thumbnail size, Today, Read and Learn-index read as one family of beige. "no more pages that look the same" is only half met. The teal Begin button is the most generic object in the set.

**Graft from A:**
- the 研ぎ出し polish reveal;
- the Me book with kintsugi seams, the seal calendar and the hand-copied line;
- the localised Kanken and part-of-speech strings.

**Worst problems in A:** the demoted sky; the crowded Today; day rooms that read alike.

## B 夜の文机 Night Desk: 45

B's night is the most "cyberpunk meets washi" thing in the review. On `words-night` the plate is a phosphor HUD on 藍, and every glyph on it is real: 記憶, 憶測, 記録, 暗記, 〜わけではない. On `today-night` a teal trace runs from 記憶 down to 記/憶 and on to 言 己 忄 意, with rain behind it. That trace is "the recursive quality of the language" drawn as light, and it is his 08-05 "word → kanji → radical" in one picture.

**B throws away the word sky.** Today is a framed plate, and there is no field of his words anywhere. By day it is, in its own words, "still mostly beige". He rejected the always-dark lacquer sheet ("too dark… too hard to read"), and B's identity lives mostly at night. Learn-index and done (`learn-day`, `done-day`) are navy slabs inside a cream app, which is the "feels like two products" jump the 10-07 review already flagged. The grade dock covers the last lines of the passage on the back.

**Graft from B:**
- the light trace from word to kanji to parts on the day's word;
- the Words HUD at night;
- the reader's resume-at-sentence-N with a scan-line under the voiced word.

**Worst problems in B:** the word sky is gone; day Today is beige; the navy rooms make it read as two products.

## C 生きた本棚 Living Shelf: 49.5

C is the only concept that does what message 3 says: it keeps his vibe and upgrades it. **His sky is now the room.** On `today-day`, his own words float on parallax planes with a 朱 tick under each due word, and 推進 is the hero. One line of wonder sits under it: "A small bird, 隹, hides inside both kanji." The verb is "Follow the bird". That sentence is the whole brief: fall in love with "the recursive quality of the language". It is also the most shareable moment in the review. `motion-frames` carries it through: 推進 → 推 → 隹 → the 40-kanji family of 隹 with "21 of its 40 kanji are already in your words." For a Kanken 1級 aspirant, that is the hook. At night (`words-night`) the plate glows phosphor with 隹 lit like a sign, and it has as much Ghost in the Shell energy as B.

Each room has its own material (woodblock and spines, lacquer stage, drafting plate, stab-bound book), so it passes the thumbnail test better than A. The session close, "The surface is clear.", is pure Dōgen, and it offers a next door with a picture. The card front is still and leak-free, and "Show English" sits behind a tap on the back. (his 10-04 ask).

What he would hit:
1. **The sky may be noise.** About 80 words is a lot. The self-critique wants a cap at 40 of his own words, and I agree.
2. **The shelf spines truncate their titles.** `read-day` shows 都市の…, AI時代…, 造化三… cut off at the fold. He has said "a truncated gloss is a broken gloss", and he wants the shelf "like readign a newspaper or magazine", sortable by topic and level. Spines are charming but carry less information than A's or D's rows.
3. **Borrowed titles** ("The stage", 舞台) he must learn.
4. **No sound, no haptics, no wrong-answer moment.** C is the most alive concept, yet it has no samurai.

**Graft from C:** whatever wins, these three belong in it:
- the day's word with its one sentence of wonder, floating in his own sky;
- the single glyph carried by match-cut across every room;
- "The surface is clear." with a next door.

**Worst problems in C:** sky density; truncated spines and a shelf below the fold; no wrong-answer moment.

## D 白紙 Hakushi: 41

D is the cleanest and the most organised. It is "quick and punchy, clean and organized", which he asked for. Two ideas in it beat every evolution:
1. **The Line.** The tab bar is a track with five stations and the due count riding on the car. Navigation becomes the "connected and flowing" thing he asked for, instead of five icons.
2. **"Today's line: 3 stops · 17 min"**, with Cards / Read / Walk the web (`today-day`). It is the clearest daily ritual of the four.

D also has the most honest wrong-answer gesture, a vermilion cut across the card on Again ("Hakuin"), and a KEPT seal that presses on Keep. The orthogonal circuit plate on Words, with GRAMMAR and CULTURE nodes, is the most "instrument" of the four. The Short 30 / Half 85 / Full 165 mock chips answer his "short medium and full version" ask word for word.

But he wrote "not from scratch though!", and D says itself that "The door (word sky) is not reimagined here… untested with him." He would read that as the "totally naive take" again. The night skyline on `today-night` looks like 8-bit pixel art, not Ōtomo. The day sheet is the most beige of all four. Buildability is low, because it is a parallel app. It is a strong graft donor, not a build.

**Graft from D:**
- the Line nav with stations and the due count;
- Today's line with stops and minutes;
- the vermilion Again cut and the KEPT seal.

The Short/Half/Full mock chips go too.

**Worst problems in D:** it breaks "keep the vibe" and the sky; the pixel skyline; the porting cost.

## Pick: C, Living Shelf, with these grafts

C keeps the door he defended, turns recursion into the signature motion, and is the one place "calm" and "addivtive" coexist on the first screen. A is the better-engineered port; C borrows its discipline.

**Grafts into C, in order of what he will notice first:**

1. **The samurai, finally.** Start from D's vermilion cut on Again and B's slash. Then build the paper-theatre samurai he "LOVE[D]", as a short authored moment, sub-second, clear and elegant: "it needs to be clear, precisse, and crispt and EASILY UNDERSTANABLE, though elegant when an answer is wrong". Whether it is "blood, or paper theatre" remains his call (open question). Without it, every concept fails his most repeated moment ask.
2. **D's Line and Today's line** replace C's three-dot strip. The track carries the due count, and the stops show minutes. Cap the sky at his 40 words so the line reads clearly beneath it.
3. **B's light trace** on the day's word: 推進 → 推/進 → 扌 隹 辶 drawn as one signal line before "Follow the bird". It is the same recursion C already has, made visible on arrival.
4. **A's Me book and togidashi.** Use the kintsugi seams, the seal calendar and the hand-copied line inside C's stab-bound book, and A's polish band as the Learn reveal. Use A's localised Kanken and POS strings.
5. **Fix the shelf.** Keep the woodblock lead. Under it, put horizontal rows with full titles, a level seal and "holds N of your words", as in A's and D's rows. Keep the spines as a secondary view. Add one hairline sort control for topic and level, never two rows of pills.
6. **Night from B.** Use the lamp-lit woodblock prints and the HUD Words plate. Keep day legible, and keep Learn and done in the same world, with no navy island.
7. **Voice and sound.** Kore narration on every article and card back, with B's scan-line. Haptic ticks on grade (B already calls `navigator.vibrate(8)`).
