# Judge 4: THE BAR panel

*道元 · 宮本武蔵 · 芭蕉 · 白隠 · 大友克洋 · 押井守 · 宮崎駿 · 今敏. We looked at both sheets, the motion frames and full-resolution shots (today-night, words-night, read-day/night) for each concept, and read every SPEC and its self-critique. We were told to be severe. Nobody kneels yet. Two of the concepts come close.*

**Panel pick for the evolution: A 磨き, with C's Today and Read grafted in.** A wins by one point, and only because it is the most complete. C has the single best moment of all four concepts.

## A 磨き Migaki

**道元.** The card is the practice: a washi card on a lacquer stage, nothing around it but トンボ marks. The 研ぎ出し band polishes the readings into view, and the session close reads "Twelve cards, whole." with the words laid out as one field instead of a pile of stats. That close is the nearest any concept comes to practice-realisation. I would bow, but I would not kneel.
**武蔵.** Today (`today-night`) has the most clutter of any screen in the four concepts. A vertical 研ぐ, a 縦書き passage, a stroke and Kanken meta column, a six-car train, a four-station line and a Begin button all ask for the eye. Cut the meta column; 研 = 石 + 开 belongs one tap away. The six train cars also come close to "two rows of grey pills".
**芭蕉.** The Read lead: Ise in woodblock with its title on a paper 短冊, and the same print at night with its lanterns lit. That stays with you. It is 不易流行 done literally: the frame holds and the light changes.
**白隠.** The popup is honest and useful: 反復 with 反 and 復 decomposed (厂+又, 彳+复), "also in a story on your shelf", and 式年遷宮 rebuilt every 20 years, "repetition as the way a thing stays new". That truth lands. The SPEC also admits the grade intervals are illustrative. Good. Now make them real.
**大友.** The Words plate at night (`words-night`) holds a paused frame: grid, polar ring, traces, passage, grammar and culture boxes. The density is earned. The walk 研ぐ→研→石→磨 with KanjiVG strokes writing in order is a proper mechanical cut.
**押井.** By night, phosphor Begin, the 縦看板 room sign lit amber and the city at the horizon line. It is restrained HUD truth. Rain falls only on Read. I want it at least felt under Today.
**宮崎.** The lacquer tab bar is black even by day, and it weighs on a morning screen. The washi fibre is lovely, but the paper does not breathe enough. Little life sits in the corners.
**今敏.** The match-cut journey (motion frames 1→6) is the strongest edit in the set: the glyph travels, re-centres and returns you "Back to today" at the exact door. That is meaning carried by the cut.

**Reasoning.** A is the most finished and the most buildable. Its tokens map one-to-one onto the corridor's TOKENS V2 fence, and its room CSS files are already split per room. It keeps everything John defended: the sky, the woodblocks, Mincho, 藍 and washi. Its weakness is restraint turning into crowding: Today says too much, and its sky is a band of decoration rather than a living field.

**Graft into any winner:** (1) the Ise lead print with its 短冊 cartouche, plus the same print at night with lanterns lit; (2) the 研ぎ出し polish reveal and the "Twelve cards, whole." close with the next door; (3) the word→kanji→part walk with KanjiVG stroke order and the "Back to today" trail.
**Worst:** (1) the Today meta column and train crowding; (2) a black lacquer tab bar by day; (3) the Me record and grade intervals are sample values dressed as real.

## B 夜の文机 Night Desk

**道元.** "Today's review is done", with the DONE seal and a next door. It is clean, but the stamp is a reward, not the practice.
**武蔵.** Mono HUD text runs everywhere: "DAY 41 OF YOUR RECORD · 32 DAYS STUDIED", a side sign repeating 08.10.2026, and "562 CHARS / 13 SENTENCES / 6% N1 VOCAB" on the shelf. Half of it does nothing. The day Words plate (`words`) is a near-empty grid.
**芭蕉.** One moment stays: on Today, 記憶 with light running down to 記 and 憶 and on to 言 己 忄 意. A word opening into its roots on the front page is a haiku of structure.
**白隠.** The shared-part bracket drawn in light under 隹 on the 推進 card back is the best teaching mark in any concept: one line, and the truth lands.
**大友 / 押井.** The night Read (`read-night`) is the most cinematic frame of the four: a lamp-lit desk, a harbour window, rain slanting across the whole screen, and a navy card floating over the print. Oshii would hold that frame for eight seconds. By day, though, the same night picture heads a morning shelf, and that is a contradiction.
**宮崎.** The paper is beige and still, and nothing lives in it by day. The SPEC admits it.
**今敏.** "The word lifts out" from the passage into the sheet is a fine match-cut, but it repeats what A does.

**Reasoning.** B has the best signal idea, light that traces meaning, and the best rain. But it **removes the word sky from Today**. VISION §8 "Always" says to keep the sky, and John defended it. For an evolution that is a fidelity failure, not a matter of taste. It reads as a smart HUD laid over a calm app, not a new world.

**Graft:** (1) the light trace word→kanji→parts as the Today word's entrance; (2) the shared-part bracket in light on card backs; (3) full-screen night rain plus the lamp print as Read's night state, and "Resume at sentence 3" on the lead.
**Worst:** (1) it drops the word sky; (2) mono HUD noise and a duplicated date sign; (3) the day hero is a night print, and the grade dock covers the passage.

## C 生きた本棚 Living Shelf

**道元.** "The surface is clear." Kept 10, again 2, 6:12, and the words that will return "in new sentences". That is the session ending on a cleared surface, which is our own check 21 met word for word.
**武蔵.** Eighty sky words on a phone, each with a vermilion due dot at night, spend 朱 like small change. Cap the sky at the learner's own words, as C's SPEC already admits, and keep the dots for words due today only. The card front (`front`) leaves a third of the card empty above a plain Reveal.
**芭蕉.** "A small bird, 隹, hides inside both kanji. Follow the bird →". This one sentence is the best line in the four concepts. It is wonder and lesson at once, and it changes every day.
**白隠.** It helps, and it lands: following the bird leads to the 隹 family plate, "21 of its 40 kanji are already in your words". That is the recursive depth of Japanese shown as something you already own. This is the shareable clip VISION §7 asks for.
**大友.** The Words plate at night (`words-night`) has the richest real density: kanji in rings, parts in boxes, passage boxes, a legend and a data table under it. The day sky over a faint skyline is good. The night skyline of lit windows under the sky is better.
**押井.** The amber glow on the night sky words is close to neon pastiche. Lower it. The 縦看板 spine with its red tick is right.
**宮崎.** Read's shelf (`read-day`): real book spines whose heights are the articles' real lengths, leaning and breathing, with woodblock tops. That is a small, cared-for thing and the most delightful object in any concept. But the titles truncate (都市の…, AI時代…), and the shelf starts below the fold.
**今敏.** "One glyph carried across contexts" is the stated thread of the whole app: Today word → web → kanji → part → back to the exact line, which glints. It is the clearest edit grammar of the four.

**Reasoning.** C is the most alive and the most loving. Its Today has one word, one sentence and one button, which is purer than A's. Its Read is the most original room. But it is less finished. Learn and Me are generic: the Me horizon grid could belong to any app. The front card is underdesigned, and 80 drifting words risk the confusion John asked us to end.

**Graft:** (1) Today's single sentence of wonder plus "Follow the bird" in place of A's meta column; (2) the Read spines with real heights under the lead story; (3) the part-family grid ("21 of its 40 kanji are already in your words") and the "The surface is clear." close.
**Worst:** (1) sky noise and overspent 朱 dots at night; (2) a half-empty card front and a generic Me; (3) spine titles truncate, and the shelf sits below the fold.

## D 白紙 Hakushi

**道元.** A clean, honest station line: Cards, Read, Walk the web. The day is laid out as a route. It is practical, but practice as logistics.
**武蔵.** The most disciplined layout of the four, with nothing strewn. The tab bar as **the Line**, five stations with a car and the due count riding on it, is the best navigation idea anywhere. Every room change travels left to right.
**芭蕉.** Nothing stays. The day's word in two boxed 推 / 進 tiles is a dictionary entry, not a moment.
**白隠.** I strike here: the parts are wrong. 推 is listed as 扌 隹 亻 and 進 as 隹 亻 辶, with 亻 in both, and 復 lists 亻 as well. The SPEC admits this is "real but imperfect" deck data. In a kanji app, a wrong part is a lie at the root of the web. It fails check 15.
**大友 / 押井.** The night Today skyline of lit windows under the day's word is the strongest single Cyberpunk-Japan image: three Japans in one frame. The night Words circuit plate with orthogonal traces is HUD truth. The KEPT hanko stamping onto the slip is good weight.
**宮崎.** The year heatmap on Me is warm and real-feeling. The Read shelf is a generic two-column card grid, and the woodblocks are shrunk.
**今敏.** The match-cuts work but say nothing A and C do not.

**Reasoning.** D is the clearest system thinking: the Line, plates, station diagrams. John, however, said "not from scratch though!" and that is the frame. D rejects the sky, which he never asked to lose. It needs a full rebuild of the shell, and its data errors strike at the heart of the product.

**Graft:** (1) the Line tab bar, with stations, a sliding car and the due count; (2) the night skyline of lit windows under Today's word; (3) the KEPT hanko stamp on Keep, and the year heatmap on Me.
**Worst:** (1) wrong parts (亻 in 推, 進 and 復); (2) no word sky, against his explicit "not from scratch"; (3) a generic Read grid and a near-total rebuild.

## Collective verdict

No concept passes THE BAR yet. Each has one or two frames a master would hold: A's Ise lanterns and its walk, B's rain over the lamp desk and its 隹 bracket, C's "Follow the bird" and its spines, D's skyline. None holds on every screen.

**Pick: A 磨き as the base.** It has the best tokens, the cleanest room split into corridor CSS, the strongest Read and Learn, and full fidelity. It needs these grafts to reach the bar:

1. **Today, recomposed (from C):** the sky becomes the living field of *his* words (at most 40) on parallax planes. The day's word sits centred with C's single sentence of wonder and its "Follow the bird" door. A's meta column is cut. A's arrival (the sky words become the train) stays as the motion, but the train shrinks to the line's first station.
2. **The word's entrance (from B):** light runs from the word to its kanji and on to their parts, once, on arrival.
3. **Read (from C):** below A's Ise lead, the shelf of real spines with real heights and full vertical titles. At night, B's full-screen rain over the print.
4. **Card back (from B):** the shared-part bracket in light under 隹.
5. **Words (from C):** the part-family grid, "21 of its 40 kanji are already in your words", as the end of every walk.
6. **Session close:** keep A's "Twelve cards, whole." and add C's line "Coming back in ten minutes, in new sentences."
7. **Navigation (from D):** the Line tab bar, with stations, a car and the due count, on washi by day and lacquer only at night.
8. **City (from D):** a skyline of lit windows under Today's word at night, so all three Japans show on the front screen (check 22).
9. **Me (from D):** the year heatmap above A's book of seals, and the KEPT hanko on every Keep.
10. **Truth (from 白隠's strike):** a build gate. Every part shown must come from KanjiVG or KanjiDic2, or from the curated `PARTS` table, and be checked; grade intervals must come from FSRS; no sample number may ship unlabelled.

Musashi's last word: build A, cut a third of Today, and nothing else.
