*An independent, adversarial review of `VISION.md` and `VISION-ONE-PAGE.md` against `JOHN_10-08_VERBATIM.md` (THE BRIEF is the frame), the three banks and `CLOUD_MISSION.md` §1. Written 2026-10-08. Fixes were applied directly to the two files; nothing else was edited.*

## Verdict

The full vision was strong on structure, sourcing and the calm/clean half. It had three real faults: the one-pager left out the ambition entirely (message 10); the engaging/addictive half was weaker than the calm half, and in one place contradicted his own method; and the cyberpunk and cutting-edge halves were described less concretely than the ancient half. All three are fixed. No quote was found to be inexact.
