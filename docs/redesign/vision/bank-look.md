## How to read this

- **Times** are JST (UTC+9), year 2026. Transcript timestamps were converted from UTC. Lavish rounds carry the time the notes were delivered.
- **Markers:** **PRAISED** he liked it · **REJECTED** he disliked or refused it · **ASK** a direction or request · **RULING** a pick or lock he made.
- **Provenance tiers** (the method of the 09-23 creator-words bank):
  - **T1**: his own typed words or Lavish annotations, in a log on this Mac.
  - **T2**: quoted as his words by an agent (repo doc, commit, code comment, memory, session summary), or dictated by voice and saved verbatim by a cloud agent. The primary is not on this Mac.
  - **T3**: an agent's paraphrase ("verbatim in spirit/intent"). Direction, not wording. Do not quote T3 back to him as his words.
- **AGENT** marks text an agent wrote, quoted only as context (what he was looking at, what was asked of him).
- His typos are kept. `…` marks a cut. [Square brackets] are my notes.
- Source keys (V, FB29, R1, PIC, BOARD, CW, DL, FEEL, LED, SU, REV, cc:, cx:, repo:, mem:) are expanded in §19.

## Through-lines (my reading; the quotes below are the evidence)

1. **The same complaint for two months.** Depth, texture, contrast, "definition", relief between layers, so that nothing "blends together". §2 lists 26 entries from 08-05 to 10-08, 18 of them in his own typed words.
2. **The feel is a pair, said in one breath.** Calm, zen, mature, refined AND alive, popping, engaging, addictive: "subtle, zen, mature, refined aesthitc, but we fill it with little things like this!" (09-23); "calm balnced with the engaging soothing crisp" (10-08). And from 08-11: zen is not softness.
3. **Ancient + future, fused.** "ancient Japan meets anime meets outer space…" (08-05, T2) → "1600 Japan on a 2335 device" (08-20, T3) → "ancient japan and cutting edge tech japan and cyberpunk" and "cyberpunk meets washi" (10-08, T1). On 08-13 he was shown three neon "cyber" worlds as whole-app themes and kept one (§17). His cyberpunk is a layer fused with washi, not a neon skin.
4. **Real over decoration.** "more real, more truth in the mechanism, never more decoration" (08-13); "still feels very digital"; pictures must make sense (09-30); tests "AS FUCKIGN REAL AS POSSIBLE" (09-28).
5. **Clarity is non-negotiable.** Confusing navigation, AI wording, vague labels and redundant buttons are rejected in every round (09-17 → 10-08).
6. **Recursion** is his word for both the language and the experience: "recursive hypnosis" (08-10), "recursive immersive expereince" (09-23), recursive cards (10-04), "the recursive quality of the language and the culture" (10-08).
7. **What he praised:** the word-sky front door; the woodblock pictures; Codex's samurai animation and "little things like this"; the living-thread and return-field directions; the ink once it became a real fluid simulation; the 5 themes; Kore + Charon "for now". Full list in §15.
8. **Tensions to hold (not to settle by dropping a side):**
   - one theme everywhere, no scheme changes (08-20 law) vs "no more pages that look the same with no differnetiatno" (10-08);
   - "keep the vibe… not from scratch" vs "if you need ot rewrite it from scratch you can do that but show me both" (both 10-08);
   - English earned, behind a tap (08-06, 10-04) vs bilingual chrome by default (08-07) vs "the English setting showing japanese buttons" as a contradiction (10-08);
   - zen calm vs "addivtive", "viral hit", "massive money" (10-08).
