# Bunki: the vision on one page

*Your 10-08 brief, held whole. Your words in quotes; the rest is our reading. Full: `VISION.md`.*

## One sentence

Bunki is one beautiful, fast, honest place where Japanese shows its recursive depth (word, kanji, parts, family, passage, culture, and back) so vividly that you fall in love, come back every day, and climb to N1, your fields, your own channel and Kanken 1級.

## The paragraph

You asked for an app that is "elegant and refined but also engageble and addivtive", so that "the user" falls "in love with the depth and beauty of Japanese, with the recursive quality of the language and the culture." Ancient Japan is the ground (washi, sumi, 藍, woodblock). Cutting-edge Japan is the instrument: instant, exact, springy under the finger. Cyberpunk Japan is the night city through the paper: phosphor light, lit vertical signs, rain on the prints, every tiny number real. One memory, one web: every word a door; reading feeds cards, cards feed reading. Calm, and it pulls: every room "much much much more alive", full of little things, one moment in each made to move you, every session ending on the next door.

## Five rooms and a door

- **Door**: the word sky you defended, made of your words.
- **Today 今日**: the daily ritual, one next step at a time.
- **Read 読む**: a fresh magazine every day, full-bleed woodblocks, every article voiced.
- **Learn 学ぶ**: a stage: cards, focus, tests, each in its own section.
- **Words 辞書**: the web as a technical plate.
- **Me 私**: honest progress to your four horizons.

## The eight laws

1. Your brief is the frame; hints tune it; the bar judges it.
2. Beauty and function are one object.
3. One app, one web: same tap everywhere.
4. One language at a time: English mode means English buttons.
5. One world, distinct rooms: no two pages look alike.
6. One job per screen; nothing strewn.
7. Real Japanese, real voices, real tests; progress never faked.
8. Calm ground, living surface: the pull is the next door and the little things, never guilt.

## The ambition: "massive money and splash"

Each rival (Duolingo, WaniKani, Bunpro, Satori, Migaku, Renshuu, LingQ) owns one room; none owns the house. Duolingo gets you started; Bunki gets you fluent, and in love. People share awe, never a score: the kanji dive, their sky, *a page I couldn't read last month*. Free forever: dictionary, your cards, due reviews. One tasteful subscription for decks, voices, sensei, mocks. The App Store via the native shell already built; every licence cleared before the first yen. It earns only if it is clean end to end.

## Two tracks: "show me both"

- **B, from scratch (白紙):** keeps only the energy and content; its own draft PR.
