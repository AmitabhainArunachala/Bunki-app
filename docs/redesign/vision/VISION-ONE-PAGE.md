# Bunki: the vision on one page

*Your 10-08 brief, held whole. Your words in quotes; the rest is our reading. Full: `VISION.md`.*

## One sentence

Bunki is one beautiful, fast, honest place where Japanese shows its own recursive depth (word into kanji into parts into family into passage into culture, and back) so vividly that you fall in love with it, come back every day, and climb to N1, your fields, your own channel and Kanken 1級.

## The paragraph

You asked for an app that is "inspiring, conencted and flowing, moduluar, interactive, engaging, immersive, elegant and refined but also engageble and addivtive", so that "the user" falls "in love with the depth and beauty of Japanese, with the recursive quality of the language and the culture." Ancient Japan is the ground (washi, sumi, 藍, woodblock), cutting-edge Japan is the instrument (hairlines, exact numbers, instant response), and cyberpunk Japan is the night city glowing through the paper. Under it runs one memory and one web: every word is a door, every walk comes back, reading feeds the cards and the cards feed reading. Calm, and it pulls. Beautiful, and it works.

## Five rooms and a door

- **Door**: the word sky you defended, made of your own words.
- **Today 今日**: the daily ritual, one next step at a time.
- **Read 読む**: a magazine of full-bleed woodblocks; a reader built around a voice.
- **Learn 学ぶ**: a stage, not a list: cards, focus sittings, tests, each in its own section.
- **Words 辞書**: the web as a technical plate: word, kanji, parts, family, passages, grammar, culture.
- **Me 私**: honest progress toward your four horizons, as a book of your year.

One colour world that never changes under you; each room its own material, light and motion.

## The eight laws

1. Your brief is the frame; hints tune it; the bar judges it.
2. Beauty and function are one object.
3. One app, one memory, one web: same tap everywhere, every walk comes back.
4. One language at a time: EN means English chrome, 日本語 means Japanese; learned Japanese is never translated away.
5. One world, distinct rooms: no two pages look alike.
6. One job per screen, one obvious next step; nothing strewn, nothing waits.
7. Real and true: real Japanese, real voices, real tests, real data; progress never faked; a wrong answer lands, clear and elegant.
8. Calm ground, living detail: the pull is the next door and small authored moments, never guilt.

## Two tracks: "show me both"

- **A, evolution:** keeps the vibration (the sky, the woodblocks, Mincho, 藍, the living thread, the samurai) and changes everything that blends or confuses. Three concepts; one is built into the app.
- **B, from scratch (白紙):** keeps only the energy and the content; a standalone prototype in its own draft PR.

Same laws, same bar, same screens.
