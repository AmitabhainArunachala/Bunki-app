## 0. The answer, short

- **From day one (2026-07-27) Bunki was meant to be one living system, never a set of apps.** Dictionary, kanji dictionary, Anki-style SRS, sentence mining, reading, immersion intake and an AI conversation all share one memory of the learner, structured (his phrase) as a *"multi-dimensional, structured, recursive learning journey"* that is fast, scientific, fun, beautiful and AI-native. It was built to cure one disease: his Japanese scattered across renzo word lists, a dozen Anki decks, Kanji Garden, Todaii and YouTube, with no single place that knew what he knew.
- **On 2026-08-05 it found its look and its door**: a nihonga ground (washi, sumi, mineral pigment) carrying Akira and Ghibli energy, and a field of floating words he could fall through (the Drift, 墨流し). On 2026-08-07 he named the whole thing: *"It is all one single APP and the APP name is 回廊 KAIRO … Drift is the main entry screen and interactive graphics. Not a separate app."*
- **Six ideas recur era after era**: the word sky, the colour worlds, a corridor of distinct rooms, a sensei who remembers, reading (with listening) at the centre, and every word as a door. Behind them run five more: recursion, one woven system, an honest memory with Anki as the floor, the focus dojo, and small authored moments of play.
- **He keeps loving**: motion that hypnotises, real ink and real paper, nihonga and woodblock, the word sky, AJATT high-context cards, "push it all the way" craft, the samurai's cut.
- **He keeps rejecting**: flat, bland, low-contrast, same-looking screens (his most repeated complaint by far); anything digital, cheap or fake; confusing words and doors; silos; clutter; robotic voices; stale or thin articles; and agents who shrink his vision to the last thing he said.
- **The 10-08 brief is the founding soul restated, not a new direction.** Its key pairs sit in the August record almost word for word: *"ancient Japan meets anime meets outer space meets obsidian graph meets additive AI study app"* (08-05), *"1600 Japan on a 2335 device — as futuristic as possible while keeping the zen dojo feel"* (08-20), Hakuin and Dogen (08-11 and again 10-08). What the build lost along the way is the voltage half (the cyberpunk world he chose, the distinct material of each room); what it never delivered is the voice and the sensei as a presence.
- **Message 10 (money, splash, rivalling every other app)** has roots as well: "product later" (07-27), "for everyone, entry level up" (08-10), "stand alone as a product later" (08-15). Money itself is new. §10 lists what the record already says about it.

## 1. What it was meant to be from the start (2026-07-27 → 08-10)

### 1.1 The problem he brought

His own words about the moment he reaches for a dictionary, the founding sentence of the whole product:

> "It is always, I don't know this perfectly and I don't want to lose it. Maybe I know the word or have heard it and the definition is close but if it's not instant and clear then I open the app and save it." (07-27, T2, `docs/convergence/JAPANESE_LEARNING_OS_CODEX_V1_FREEZE_2026-07-27.md:67-70`)

The rest of the 07-27 evidence ledger (T3, same file :76-99): the dictionary app is his dominant capture tool, he "almost never reviews there; its study experience is boring"; he loves Anki when he uses it and notices "big gains"; his kanji app feels like it is "never finishing". Two AIs, working blind to each other, named the same disease: **state fragmentation**. Claude's 07-27 review counted at least five systems holding "unreconciled shards" of his learner state (`docs/convergence/BUNKI_WORKING_SPEC_2026-07-27.md:320-325`, T4).

His non-negotiables, as the Codex freeze tagged them **USER-STATED** (`JAPANESE_LEARNING_OS_CODEX_V1_FREEZE_2026-07-27.md:227-279`):

1. One continuous system: "They cannot remain adjacent mini-apps with separate notions of 'known.'"
2. Fast capture and low lag: a lookup "must never wait for a full AI response".
3. Both review and immersion.
4. AI-native conversation, "not a decorative chatbot tab."
5. "The experience should be beautiful, fun, and immersive."

### 1.2 The founding shape

- **U1** (T2 phrase): one dictionary / kanji dictionary / Anki-SRS sentence-mining app, structured as a *"multi-dimensional, structured, recursive learning journey"* (:58-61).
- **U2**: AI conversation that develops "a precise understanding of the learner's Japanese" and creates "bifurcating and trifurcating learning journeys for deep memorization and immersion" (:63-65). This is where the first name came from (1.5).
- **U8**: review, natural resurfacing, generated material and new learning together, at a *"hyper fast and hyper flexible iterative rate"* (:96-99).
- **AJATT lineage**, his words: *"huge fan and believer in Ajatt method… a web scraper YouTube mcp that just ingest stuff from all over, rss feed, etc."* (07-27, T2, `docs/convergence/BUNKI_CONVERGENCE_ROUND1_2026-07-27.md:26-28`).
- Claude's founding thesis (T4, `BUNKI_WORKING_SPEC_2026-07-27.md:14-23`): "Every existing Japanese-learning tool treats the language as a **list** … Japanese is natively a **graph** … **The product is the closed loop.**" This is the seed of the 10-08 "recursive quality of the language".
- Laws both AIs wrote and he never contradicted (T4): capture creates no review debt; partial knowledge is first-class; FSRS schedules, AI only proposes; every judgment about the learner cites evidence.
- A line often quoted as his "frozen words" is **not his**: "Nothing meaningful you encounter is lost; not everything becomes homework; what does become practice returns in the right form, context, and intensity." It is Codex's product promise (`JAPANESE_LEARNING_OS_CODEX_V1_FREEZE_2026-07-27.md:338-339`), T4.

### 1.3 The founding feel: a living wallpaper

The single strongest visual signal of the founding week is his comment on Kanji Garden's knowledge wallpaper (his whole kanji inventory coloured by knowledge state, with a 14-month time slider):

> "the most visually cool thing in all these environments… But it is not even interactive at all." (07-27, T2, `BUNKI_CONVERGENCE_ROUND1_2026-07-27.md:32-34`)

Claude's 07-27 spec answered: "Bunki's centerpiece is that artifact **alive**": a constellation where node brightness is memory strength, tappable, with a history scrubber (T4, `BUNKI_WORKING_SPEC_2026-07-27.md:184-198`). The same section set the first register: typography first (Shippori Mincho), first-class furigana, ink-and-paper, one vermilion accent, generous *ma*, "a kanji page should feel like a museum card, not a spreadsheet row", "No confetti, no XP" (T4).

This wallpaper is the ancestor of the word sky (3.1).

### 1.4 The founding learner

- 07-27 probe (T3, `BUNKI_WORKING_SPEC_2026-07-27.md:327-346`): component analysis strong, listening and inference about N2+, formal written grammar about N3 (missed につき and どころか). "Aspiration: upper Kanken."
- Immersion then: about 2 h a day (formerly 6-8 h): TOLAND VLOG, 中田敦彦のYouTube大学, ダイゴロー, NHKマイあさ (T3).
- His Anki, recovered by agents (agent-measured, GitHub issue #32): 8,331 notes, 47,400 reviews over 992 study days, 2009 to 2024, dormant since 2023. "His 209-card deck is textbook Massive-Context Cloze Deletion." His deck list is "an archaeology of methodology reboots" (RTK, Lazy Kanji, Japanese MCD Pro, "2024 NIHONGO", "2025 漢字検定"), each reboot orphaning its history (T4, `BUNKI_WORKING_SPEC_2026-07-27.md:250-268`).
- His own story, told for the first time at length on 10-04 and weighted "10 stars above anything else" (T1 transcript, `docs/srs/LEARNER_VISION_2026-10-04.md:8-70`): started in 2006, nearly failed a university semester, a six-week ryugaku in 2007, five years in Japan teaching yoga "mostly dokugaku", left in 2014, "basically stopped studying Japanese until I met Atsumi in 2023". Goals: JLPT N1 "as soon as possible"; Kanken 1級, "the summit of the, the pinnacle"; "a Japanese YouTube channel where I can speak on a variety of topics, both simple and complex"; "a master's degree in a few different topics in Japanese". His most effective method: "to read Japanese books with audio transcript listening at the same time".

### 1.5 The two names

- **分岐 Bunki** (07-27): "branch point — a railway term; gifts the visual language of a branching metro map" (T4, `BUNKI_WORKING_SPEC_2026-07-27.md:7-8`). The name came from U2's branching journeys. Parked alternates: 木霊 Kodama, 歩み Ayumi.
- **回廊 KAIRO** (08-07), his words:

> "It is all one single APP and the APP name is 回廊 KAIRO … And the entire kairo app has all, or most at least, of the entire Bunki app woven in it. Drift is the main entry screen and interactive graphics. Not a separate app. That all needs to be crystal clear." (2026-08-07, T2, quoted in GitHub issue #49, AmitabhainArunachala/Bunki-app)

  The Wayfinder map's gloss (agent, issue #32): "a corridor that circulates and returns — recursive, unlike 分岐 which branches away." The same issue #49 notes "the operator once wrote 回廊/回路". 回路 (also かいろ) means *circuit*. The question was never closed. The repository is still `AmitabhainArunachala/Bunki-app`, and the rename plan's "Decision requested: FIRE · FIRE+REPO · HOLD" line carries no recorded answer (`docs/build-evidence/renkan/proposals/RENAME_49_BUNKI_TO_KAIRO_PLAN.md`).
  My reading, flagged: the homophone holds the 10-08 brief in two characters. 回廊 is the roofed cloister of a temple (ancient Japan); 回路 is a circuit (cutting-edge tech Japan). Both circulate and return.

### 1.6 The 08-10 statement (the fullest early statement of scope and feel)

Agent-rendered as "the product, in the operator's own words" (T2 list, `docs/briefs/KAIRO_BUNKI_BRIEF_2026-08-10.md:7-18`):

> A fully comprehensive, all-inclusive Japanese app — everything every other Japanese app has, combined into ONE flowing sequence: a ~70,000-word dictionary that works like a paper dictionary; a full synonym dictionary, bar none; a full kanji dictionary and a grammar dictionary; an amazing Anki-grade SRS, all of Anki's function, simplified; Duolingo-class lessons, for everyone, entry level up; JLPT N5→N1 and Kanken up to level 1; a reader in the class of Satori Reader / Shinobi / Todai; AI woven in, living IN the app: you speak to it, it judges your level, creates custom readings and articles at your level, adjusts the SRS to your level, runs many layers of testing.
>
> **The experience: mesmerizing — recursive hypnosis that pulls you in; extreme focus and concentration but also ease; aesthetic precision, really great aesthetic vibes; a full flow; it makes sense; it is intuitive; all elements woven together.**

Two days earlier, the v11 handoff (07-30, T4, `docs/handoffs/BUNKI_SITES_V11_INTERACTION_REDESIGN_HANDOFF_2026-07-30.md:63-67`) had already set the rule the 10-08 brief repeats: "Every complex capability must open from a clean, minimal, almost meditative surface. Complexity appears progressively through deliberate taps; it must never overwhelm the default screen." and "Do not delete this vision merely to simplify the UI. Simplify the *journey through it*." The verdict on that prototype: "The present experience is unacceptable." (T3, :71)

### 3.3 The corridor of rooms

"Rooms" is mostly agent vocabulary. His words: "one single APP", "the dojo, or study room", "a kanji-alone portion of the app", the writing room's controls, "the floating world first drift page".

## 11. The soul, in one paragraph

Bunki is a corridor through the Japanese language that circulates and returns (回廊). It was made for one learner who has walked toward Japanese for twenty years, lost it, and come back, so that nothing he meets is ever lost again: every word opens into its kanji, every kanji into its parts and its family, every sentence into the article and the voice it came from, and every path loops back to where he stood. Underneath it is exact. Anki is the floor, FSRS keeps the time, the sensei remembers every conversation and quietly weaves what he missed into the next card, the next article and the field of words at the door, and no mastery is ever faked or softened. On the surface it is ancient Japan carrying future voltage: washi, sumi and mineral pigment, woodblock light, the energy of Akira and Ghost in the Shell, a floating world of his own words that moves like ink on water, rooms that each feel like their own place, and small moments of play inside a calm, mature instrument. Its test is not a green suite. It is whether he falls in love with the depth and recursion of the language, and cannot stop coming back.
