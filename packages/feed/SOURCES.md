# Source catalog review — 2026-09-10

This expansion adds 24 distinct publishers/institutions to the original 13, preserving all original source IDs, publisher IDs and five Asahi channels. The resulting catalog contains 41 channels from 37 publishers/institutions. The nine new personal-device routes bring configured metadata coverage to 21 channels from 17 publishers; 20 channels remain publisher links. Counts describe configured capabilities, not live availability. Publication timestamps and request success are measured independently. No publisher source has a full-reader adapter in this package.

The first `notes` entry describes each added source in Japanese. Topics include regional reporting, arts, literature, history, travel, food, economics, technology, science, the environment and Japanese learning. A museum review, library reference case or graded book is not represented as breaking news. Additional sections from a publisher do not inflate the publisher count. Publisher identity uses the named publishing organization/institution, not every topic or site; for example, Pen uses the current CE Media House identity found in its official terms.

## Per-operation limits and future full readers

Only `personal-fetch` and `display-metadata` are enabled for reviewed personal routes. Metadata retention/sync, excerpts, body display/retention/sync, AI transformation, synthesized/publisher audio and images remain separate unenabled operations. `server-ingest` is explicitly denied by this personal-device adapter. Sync references contain identity and a canonical link, without publisher titles or bodies.
