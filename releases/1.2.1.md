# Bunki / KAIRO 1.2.1

Planned release date: 2026-10-14 (Asia/Tokyo). Publication is established by the GitHub Release and live build identity.

## Changes

- **The decks open in the focus room in every layout.** On 10-13 the
  learner asked for the review screen to be "very clear and smooth with NO
  CLUTTER at all", and was shown the focus room. 1.2.0 shipped it only behind
  **Settings → Layout → New (test)**, which is off by default, so the
  everyday app kept the old deck screen. Now every deck the app opens (N2,
  N1, fields, looked-up words) uses it, whatever the layout:
  - while a card is up there is no top bar, no tab bar and no report button;
  - tapping the right half of the screen turns the card, and the left half
    stays still, so a tap while reading never gives the answer away. The
    hint for a deck's first sittings now says so ("Recall the meaning, then
    tap the right side");
  - after the reveal every kanji carries its reading, and a **Readings**
    switch returns the clean text without moving a line. Readings no longer
    run into each other (連戦連勝, 賠償金) or past the edge of the page
    (交渉 at a line's start): each line's readings are seated apart, and a
    word with a reading is kept on one line, before and after the reveal
    alike, so its reading is never drawn over a word split across two lines
    (小村寿太郎). The passage is set ragged rather than stretching a short
    line;
  - the four grades are colour coded, as chosen on 10-08: Again red, Hard
    amber, Good green, Easy blue;
  - the close says how many words were met, in how many minutes, and how
    many needed a second look, keeps the recall rate as one quiet line under
    it, and opens today's reading as the one door forward.
- Made while bringing the focus room to every deck, so that it keeps what the
  decks already promised:
  - **Remove** in the session bar reaches 44px, like every other control.
  - The card is plain paper: no texture under the readings.
  - 焦点 folds the sentences around the target to exactly two lines (a sliver
    of a third showed).
  - The level figure and Remove use colours certified at 4.5:1.
  - The deck's English wording is the same in every layout.
- Deck player fixes, found while checking the focus room at every width:
  - **A word that wraps across two lines no longer catches taps meant for
    the words around it.** Its 44px reach was centred between the two lines
    and covered both; it now keeps only its printed letters.
  - **An opened 焦点 group keeps its ⋯ after a repaint** (for example on the
    way back from a kanji page), so it can be folded again, and the passage
    keeps the learner's place in its reading window.
  - Opening a 焦点 group that pushes the word and its definition under the
    grade bar now gives the passage its reading window, as at the reveal.
  - After the reveal, the fold rows under the definition (英語, 出典 and the
    rest) are no longer left cut by the grade bar. The scroll that clears them
    was worked out in fractions of a pixel but the page scrolls in whole ones,
    so at some text widths a row ended about a pixel across the bar. The
    scroll is now planned in whole pixels.

  The standalone study pages (`decks/kotoba-mine/release/study*.html`) are
  rebuilt with these fixes and otherwise behave as before: the focus room
  needs the app.

- The five places (Review · Bookshelf · Dictionary · Tests · Progress) stay
  behind the Layout switch.

## Release tooling

- When a CI gate fails, the battery now also annotates the failing checks'
  own lines (up to six), so they can be read from the check run when the
  job log and its artifacts cannot be reached.

## Data and compatibility

No learner-data, card-ID, backup-format or FSRS change, and no migration. A
deck's preferences may gain the optional `readings` field (`on` or `off`) in
its existing `bunki-cloze:prefs:v3:<deck>` key, as in 1.2.0's new layout;
earlier versions ignore it. Learner-data epoch stays 1, and rolling back to
1.2.0 or 1.0.1 is compatible. The scheduler remains FSRS-6 through ts-fsrs
5.4.1 with `bunki-fsrs6-r090-defaults-v1`.

## Verification

- The `places-layout` gate gains a section for the current layout: a fresh
  device opens a deck from Learn into the focus room (no top bar, tab bar or
  report button while a card is up; the left half still and the right half
  turning; the Readings switch on the back), and the four grade words are
  four distinct hues at 4.5:1 or more on their pads.
- Existing deck gates assumed the old deck screen. They were updated only
  where the learner's 10-13 decisions changed the design, and each update
  asserts the new design as strictly, with the decision quoted beside it:
  - `deck-player-browser` (verify-kotoba-mine): the tap hint names the right
    half; the head draws only the level (the kind, topic and state chips are
    built but not drawn); the card has one hairline edge and the target is
    bold ink; the grade words are OKLCH hues 28 · 75 · 150 · 255; readings
    drawn above the kanji are measured for contrast as readings.
  - `player-dock`: Reveal is the right half of the screen; Undo stands 10px
    off the foot plus the safe area exactly once, on its opaque band.
  - `player-presentation`: the card screen has no language or world switch
    and no report entry, so the reading-pose comparisons use the card's own
    Readings switch; the report entry's accessible names are checked on the
    deck home; the surname rebuild is checked on the way back from the kanji
    page.
  - `redesign-docks`: on a deck card the report button is hidden, not visible.
  - `redesign-language`: a deck card is a quiet room (no tab bar), like 復習's zen.
- Local on e512e337's built site, Chromium, one verifier at a time: deck
  player (all checks), player-dock (80 cases), player-presentation (115),
  places-layout (92), redesign-docks (12 Chromium cases), UI language (both
  languages), skip-standalone, accessibility, redesign foundation,
  report-entries (77 Chromium checks) and corridor (259). The N2/N1 tour
  passed its six tours but logged one 404 console error, which the live
  1.2.0 build logs the same way on this machine. WebKit is not installed
  locally; CI's WebKit shards cover it. Full CI admission is required on the
  pull request.
- Not yet tested on the learner's own phone. Automated runs use Chromium;
  CI's WebKit shards cover Safari's engine but are not a physical device.
