# Bunki / KAIRO 1.3.0-rc.2

Development candidate prepared 2026-11-09 (Asia/Tokyo). This is not a published release.

## Changes

Use the shared canonical Nihongo engine with Japanese-only audio admission. Complete Japanese prose fields are selected from current source before recording preparation, packaging, acquisition and playback. English translations remain visible. English, mixed-language, unassigned and notation fields are excluded from audio without truncating or changing their source.

Unbound audio fallbacks are unavailable where current Japanese source and profile authority cannot be established. Ordinary reading and review remain available. Complete ElevenLabs/Kore recordings and listening acceptance are outstanding; synthetic clips establish software behavior only.

## Data and compatibility

Learner-data epoch remains 1. No learning history is migrated, deleted or reset. Card/task identities, reducer and replay dependencies remain pinned. The existing canonical sync, grade, Undo, retry and durable review contracts are preserved. This candidate is prepared on a separate branch and uses an isolated test learner.

## Verification

Source selection independently recomputed 51,082 Japanese placements and 50,215 source references from 77,992 fields. Exclusions are 18,529 English, 7,066 mixed, 578 notation and 737 unassigned placements. These are text coverage counts, not recorded media counts.

Canonical producer and runtime component tests, independent negative controls and native acquisition/play/grade/reopen checks are recorded by exact source. Final composed gates, export/pin verification, built-browser qualification and hosted CI determine this candidate's admission; pending checks are not reported as passed. The earlier 1.3.0-rc.1 preview remains available separately. Physical iPhone behavior and actual paired Mac/Bunki sync remain unverified. No paid synthesis, credential change, production release or deployment is included.
