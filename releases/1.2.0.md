# Bunki / KAIRO 1.2.0

Planned release date: 2026-10-14 (Asia/Tokyo). Publication is established by the GitHub Release and live build identity.

## Changes

- **A new layout to try, as a test.** **Me → Settings → Layout** now offers
  **Current** and **New (test)**. Current is the default and is unchanged: the
  app opens exactly as 1.1.0 does until the switch is turned. The choice is kept
  on this device, like the colour theme, and can be switched back at any time.
  A link can also choose it: `?layout=places` for the new layout,
  `?layout=current` for the current one.
- **The new layout has five places, each named for what is inside it.** The
  bottom bar reads **復習 Review · 本棚 Bookshelf · 辞書 Dictionary · 試験 Tests ·
  記録 Progress**, in the order of a study day. The old names (Today, Read,
  Learn, Words, Me) were coined by an agent brief on 10-08 and were never chosen
  by the learner.
  - **Review** holds every card in one place. The day's saved words lead with
    **Begin · N cards** (and minutes, once the learner's own pace supports them).
    Below are the lists, the N2, N1, fields and looked-up-words decks, timed
    practice, **Saved words & lists** and **Browse cards**. Before, saved words
    were reviewed from Today and the decks from Learn.
  - **Bookshelf** opens with **Today's reading**: the unread article holding
    the most of the learner's own words.
  - **Dictionary** opens with the search box, then the word of the day with the
    part its kanji share.
  - **Tests** holds the JLPT practice tests and their lengths, guided practice,
    lessons, sentence practice and the JLPT & Kanken lists. Tests are kept
    apart from the cards because they are taken without help.
  - **Progress** holds the year in Japanese, statistics and Settings. Settings
    now holds the review pace, record sync, export and import, bug reports and
    the note to the builder directly, instead of doors that jumped to Today.
  - **Saved words & lists** (from Review or Progress) holds the card counts
    and forecast, every list with its words, the tutor's quiz, saved sentence
    practice and saved notes.
- **The opening screen in the new layout.** Its one button reads **Review · N
  cards**. Tapping the symbol opens a short menu of the five places, each with
  a line saying what is inside, and the tutor stays in its corner.
- **One way around.** In the new layout the top bar of every room holds only
  Back and search. The colour theme and the interface language are in
  Settings; the opening screen keeps its colour seal. English buttons are set
  in sentence case.
- **The focus room, in the new layout's decks.** While a card is up there is
  no top bar, no tab bar and no report button. Tapping the right half of the
  screen turns the card; the left half stays still, so a tap while reading
  never gives the answer away. After the reveal every kanji carries its
  reading, and a **Readings** switch returns the clean text without moving a
  line. A tapped word stays lit while its meaning is open. The close says how
  many words were met, in how many minutes, and how many needed a second look,
  and opens today's reading as the one door forward.
- **The standalone study pages** (`decks/kotoba-mine/release/study*.html`) are
  rebuilt from the updated deck player. They behave as before: the focus room
  needs the app and appears only there.

## Data and compatibility

No learner-data, card-ID, backup-format or FSRS change, and no migration. Both
layouts read and write the same record in the same way; nothing is moved or
copied when the layout changes. The layout choice is a device preference in
`localStorage` key `kairo-layout` (`places`, or absent for the current
layout), outside the learner record and outside backups. In the new layout a
deck's preferences may gain an optional `readings` field (`on` or `off`) in its
existing `bunki-cloze:prefs:v3:<deck>` key; earlier versions ignore it.
Learner-data epoch stays 1 and rolling back to 1.1.0 or 1.0.1 is compatible:
an older version opens in its own layout with the same cards and record. The
scheduler remains FSRS-6 through ts-fsrs 5.4.1 with
`bunki-fsrs6-r090-defaults-v1`.

This release contains 1.1.0's changes. Merge and release 1.1.0 (pull request
#132) first.

## Verification

- **New gate `places-layout`** (`prototypes/corridor/tools/verify-places-layout.mjs`):
  real Chromium at 390 × 844 on the built site, with a 140-word record
  imported through the app's own importer. With the layout switched on through
  Settings it checks the five names on the tab bar and that no two labels touch;
  that every room's top bar holds only Back and search and lights its own
  station; Review's Begin, deck row and tab badge giving one number; every deck
  and list in Review and no tests there; Begin starting the review and leaving
  it returning to Review; Saved, its lists, Browse and the backup door, and each
  way back; Tests holding the JLPT tests, guided practice, lessons, sentence
  practice and level lists, and each way back to Tests; today's reading opening
  its article; the day's word in the Dictionary and leaving with a search;
  Progress → Saved and Settings (pace, sync, export, import, builder note);
  the opening screen's Review button and menu; the focus room's right-half
  turn, readings on the back with no line moving, and the Readings switch; no
  page errors. It also checks that switching back restores the current layout.
- **The current layout is unchanged:** every existing gate runs against the
  default layout and passes without any edit to its assertions.
- Local, before the pull request: corridor lint, Prettier, `release:verify`,
  the CI contract tests, the frozen-deck rebuild check, and the existing
  corridor, accessibility, foundation, Today-queue, doors and deck-player
  verifiers on the built site. Full CI admission is required on the pull
  request.
- Not yet tested on the learner's own phone. Automated runs use Chromium; the
  WebKit shards in CI cover Safari's engine but are not a physical device.
