# Bunki / KAIRO 1.3.0-rc.3

## Changes

- A revealed shared vocabulary card places the passage beside its explanation on wide screens. Rotating on a short phone keeps the grades and the audio control reachable. The unpaired study room also retains its reading seat as its columns reflow.
- In short landscape viewports the reader places the illustrated cover beside the article so the first Japanese lines are visible on entry.
- The shared study host accounts for pending answers across the complete local outbox when a paired installation ends. Pending work can be reported after a restart.
- The legacy player protects unreadable or externally removed deck records. Deck shortcuts respect browser commands and focused controls, and the private collection's Japanese interface accepts Space to reveal.
- Repeating a word inside a timed practice block records practice evidence after its scheduled grade. The countdown leaves adjacent actions available.
- The desktop foreground journal distinguishes delayed reads from uncertain writes, validates commit and replay receipts, and prepares batches with less repeated work.
- Startup requests independent validators together before opening learner storage. The kanji compound index computes sort keys once per bucket entry.

- Build assembly starts each generated core module before sealing the artifact. An incompatible workspace installation now fails the build.
