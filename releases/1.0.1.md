# Bunki / KAIRO 1.0.1

Planned release date: 2026-10-13 (Asia/Tokyo). Publication is established by the GitHub Release and live build identity.

## Changes

- **Generated articles can finish.** The article room (私の読み物) asks the tutor
  for 2,000–12,000 characters in one reply, but every tutor request was cut off
  after 10 seconds. No provider writes an article that fast, so each request
  failed and the room asked the learner to check the connection. The article
  room now waits up to 10 seconds plus the requested length at 25 tokens per
  second, capped at ten minutes: about 2, 5½ and 10 minutes for short, medium
  and long. Cancel still stops it at any time. Chat, word tutor, examples,
  quiz, coach, sentence mining and every other tutor request keep 10 seconds.
- **Release check repair.** The N2/N1 deck tour leaves the app before switching
  the test browser offline, so an article still loading in the background no
  longer fails the check at random. The app is unchanged by this repair.

## Data and compatibility

No learner-data, card-ID, backup-format, storage-key or FSRS change. The
scheduler remains FSRS-6 through ts-fsrs 5.4.1 with
`bunki-fsrs6-r090-defaults-v1`. Learner-data epoch stays 1 with no migration,
and rolling back to 1.0.0 is compatible: 1.0.0 reads everything 1.0.1 writes.

Update as before: open the app online, close all its windows, and reopen.
Do not clear website data.

## Verification

A new contract check in `verify-corridor-ai` pins both time budgets: 10 seconds
for every interactive surface and 130/330/600 seconds for the three article
lengths. The existing check that a never-answering tutor request ends in the
quiet failure line at about 10 seconds is unchanged.

Before this release, the N2/N1 deck tour failed intermittently because an
in-flight article request returned 503 when the network was cut: 4 of 8 local
Chromium runs logged it. With the tour change, none of 7 did (3 on the 1.0.0
source, 4 on this release).

The article budget is a time limit, not a speed guarantee: a provider that
writes more slowly than 25 tokens per second can still be stopped at the cap.
No physical-iPhone run is claimed. The full CI receipt is attached by the
publisher after admission.
