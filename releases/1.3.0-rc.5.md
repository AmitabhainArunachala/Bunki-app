# Bunki / KAIRO 1.3.0-rc.5

## Changes

Today offers study and reading together. A returning learner can continue the deck they last reviewed when work is available. Reading size follows Japanese text through the passage, dictionary, saved words and shared study card. Saved-word review selects examples by reading and returns to the occurrence the learner saved. Shared study opens its dictionary alongside its grammar materials and offers a retry if those materials fail to open. Paired study attempts a bounded upload when a page goes into the background and schedules another attempt after a transient failure.

## Data and compatibility

The learner-data epoch remains 1. Card IDs, FSRS parameters, backups and review payloads retain their existing formats. The reading-size preference is device local. Earlier local records can reopen, including saved words whose original passage no longer matches. Rollback is compatible.
