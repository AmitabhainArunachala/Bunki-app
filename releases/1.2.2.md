# Bunki / KAIRO 1.2.2

Planned release date: 2026-11-08 (Asia/Tokyo). Publication is established by the GitHub Release and live build identity.

## Changes

- **The Rajōmon encounter, the first kanji-world example.** The sheet for the
  kanji 門 now carries a door, "Visit Rajōmon · preview" (羅城門へ · 試作). It
  opens a private, ungraded encounter: a picture of Rajōmon, the character 門,
  the word 正門 with its reading せいもん, then two recall steps with the cues
  taken away (type the reading, then the meaning), with a wrong answer named
  plainly and a reveal that never counts as practice. "Return" (戻る) closes
  it and puts the learner back on the exact same sheet, scroll position and
  focused button. Nothing is written to the learner's record, and any other
  change of page closes the encounter.
- **How a learner reaches it.** Open the kanji sheet for 門 from any
  exploration room: the Reading shelf's tools → Kanji index (search, the
  Kodansha-number lens, or the kanji lists), the reader, search or the
  reference library. The door does not appear inside graded rooms (review,
  lessons, tests, mock, guided practice, decks) or during a learning-source
  visit, so it never interrupts scored work.
- **The picture is under judgement.** The scene (`kanji-world/rajomon-r1.png`)
  and this encounter are the first kanji-world example; John judges the picture
  before publication. The door is labelled "preview" (試作) for that reason.
  Ported from the campaign commit 5003737c4 onto main.

## Data and compatibility

No learner-data change. Card identities, FSRS parameters, backups and the
learner-data epoch are unchanged (epoch 1, migration none, rollback
compatible). The encounter reads two new static release assets
(`kanji-world/rajomon-content.json`, precached, and the 5 MB scene PNG,
verified and cached by the service worker on first use) and writes nothing.
