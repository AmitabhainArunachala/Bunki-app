# Bunki / KAIRO releases

Published history and retained packages:
[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Amitabhainarunachala/Bunki-app/releases).
The current candidate's version is owned by [release.json](release.json).
Each candidate adds its own completed notes under [releases/](releases/);
the release's publication status and exact deployment evidence are established
by its GitHub Release, not by the presence of a notes file.

Numbering begins with [1.0.0](releases/1.0.0.md). The preceding verified app is
recorded in [the October 12 baseline](releases/baseline-2026-10-12.json).
Earlier dated documents and branches are development history, not retroactive
product release numbers.

See [the versioning and release contract](docs/operator/VERSIONING_AND_RELEASES.md).
